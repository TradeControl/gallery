**Status:** C1 approved; C2's inventory disclosures and C3a's passive passage marks artistically rejected; the C4a filigree direction is closed without artistic sign-off; immutable E1 and the last green E2 remain reproducible technical evidence only; C4b-O1 is technically valid but artistically rejected as a wrapped thread; C4c-I1 is technically valid but artistically rejected as too longitudinal and visually subdued; C4d-D1, C4d-D2 and C4d-D3 are approved
**Implementation:** C1 remains hidden architecture; E1 profile `c4a-exterior-brooch-v1`, E2 profile `c4a-longitudinal-placement-v1`, O1 profile `c4b-exterior-optical-fibre-v1` and I1 profile `c4c-internal-arborescence-v1` remain selectable evidence; D1 profile `d1-inverted-internal-router-v1` supplies a normally invisible wall-constrained router; D2 profile `c4d-d2-router-junction-v1` connects the shared canopy route to its exact seed and records one provenance-linked output there; D3 profile `c4d-d3-independent-router-transport-v1` dispatches that same output down one deterministic route; D4 is eligible for a separate bounded brief but has not begun, and C5 remains unauthorised

## Executive finding

The accepted trunk is a convincing opaque exterior, and C1 supplies a coherent
internal carrier architecture beneath it. C2 then proved that an aperture,
trunk-local attenuation and an ownership stencil can disclose that architecture
without corrupting geometry, packet transport or the distant silhouette. None of
those technically successful methods was artistically accepted.

The negative result is more fundamental than the individual renderers. Exposing
a small, enumerable collection of components converts a potentially inexhaustible
recursive order into an inspection problem. The aperture makes this collapse
especially clear: monumentality and mystery give way to a window onto a handful
of conduits. Attenuation and stencil are quieter, but preserve the same inventory
premise. C2 therefore rejects the display of the hidden mechanism as an enumerable
anatomy; it does not establish that every transducer must be invisible.

Experiment C should instead distinguish the existence of a mechanism from the
artistic need to inventory it. C1 can remain a hidden causal architecture and a
separate engineering diagnostic. In the artwork, the graphite body can act as
reserve or negative space: a finite form implies more than it enumerates. One
singular visible transducer may nevertheless concentrate rather than diminish
that mystery if it is an artwork within the artwork, and if its form, its
relationship with the trunk and its causal response to information are conceived
together.

The technical C1, C2 and C3a records below remain valuable experimental provenance.
They establish what exists and what can be rendered; the artistic review
establishes that an inventory disclosure is not the intended language and that a
passive packet-triggered mark is not sufficiently functional. The surface C4a
maze exposed a centre/surface disconnect. A subsequent wholly internal,
depth-disclosed path is retained as a technical continuity prototype. C4a-E1 then
made the causal route itself a visible three-dimensional gold filigree object and
passed its technical gate, but human review found that its compact exterior loops
resembled a brooch attached to the trunk. It remains evidence rather than the
finished artistic solution. C4a-E2 now implements the same one-to-one interval
longitudinally as an axial shell-straddling volume. Its bounded technical gate
passed, but its one-flank presentation, form-dependent phase and sparse visibility
did not resolve the intended DNA-like artwork. The filigree direction is therefore
closed without artistic sign-off. It does not expose C1, combine independent
inputs or claim higher-order meaning.

A later reviewed brief authorised one separate C4b-O1 exterior optical-fibre
spiral. It proved a continuous, packet-revealed object-space descent, but human
review on 2026-10-18 found that the centre-running canopy stream appeared to jump
onto a pretty thread wrapped around the trunk. O1 remains technical evidence.
C4c-I1 then tested whether genuine packet events could briefly disclose different
rootward journeys inside the intact graphite while keeping the entry and resume
central. Its one-to-one ledger remains authoritative, but the visible result was
too longitudinal and subdued to establish the intended recursive root image.
C4d-D1 is the separately bounded successor and is now human-approved. D2's exact
canopy-to-router alignment is also approved as essential infrastructure, but its
construction marks are not accepted as a transducer-rune design. D3's bounded
transport experiment is also human-approved as causal infrastructure; its
construction view is not accepted as the transducer rune. D4 has not begun and
C5 is not implied.

## Established architecture

### Exterior and growth

- The active trunk is one authored chain: `planting-root → uptake → exchange →
  meaning`, followed by the eligible `crown-trunk-*` stages. At the default
  three tiers it contains seven nodes and six spans.
- `createMonumentalTrunkGeometry()` constructs one indexed loft with ten samples
  per span and 18 radial sides. Sections are mildly elliptical, aligned to the
  crown load and slightly reinforced on the loaded side.
- The default mature radii are approximately `1.42`, `1.195`, `1.011`, `0.84`,
  `0.672`, `0.538` and `0.430`. Taper below `meaning` is art-directed; upper
  radii also respect the hierarchy's load-derived support radius.
- The lower loft continues slightly below the planting datum and has flat base
  and top closures. It is a closed surface, not a solid, hollow vessel or shell
  with modeled wall thickness.
- Growth rebuilds the loft at quantised 1/28 maturation steps, changes its
  station radii and reveals one contiguous indexed prefix. The top closure is
  shown only when the active chain is complete.
- The accepted graphite is an opaque `MeshPhysicalMaterial`. It has no
  transmission or alpha-based interior disclosure.

The three-tier trunk is only 1,154 vertices and 2,232 triangles. Even the
eight-tier trunk is about 4,032 triangles. This makes bounded local geometry
plausible, but does not by itself prove headroom for liners, apertures or extra
render passes; those costs still require measurement.

### Branch attachment

Every non-trunk relationship remains an independent tapered tube. For the nine
lateral edges whose parent is `meaning` or an active `crown-trunk-*` node, the
centreline starts at the exact parent-node position inside the trunk and exits
through the trunk skin. The shared deformation field keeps coincident junction
positions together.

This produces a visually strong deep overlap, not a manifold union. There is no
port, collar, Boolean join or shared surface vertex. At the default state, nine
real lateral attachments enter the trunk:

- `operations`, `finance`, `resources`;
- `process-observation`, `working-capital`;
- `resource-assurance`, `delivery-rhythm`; and
- `financial-horizon`, `productive-capacity`.

The archived Gate 2 experiment retained topology and provenance in bounded
smaller states, but the mature 406-edge state exceeded its mesher cell budget.
Its swelling, sockets and wedges also damaged the accepted engineered form.
Experiment C should not revive global implicit or Boolean meshing. Local,
parametric trunk construction remains the credible direction.

### Existing internal traffic geometry

The current internal core is structurally related to the hierarchy:

- every eligible terminal route is the exact `parentId` chain to
  `planting-root`;
- each unique edge in the eligible terminal-route union occurs once in one
  merged traffic geometry;
- a 64-sample texture row carries the current light signal for that edge; and
- independent packet records retain their own identity, route, clock and
  travelled distance.

At three tiers, 213 terminal routes reduce to 388 unique edge tubes. The merged
core is approximately 104,130 vertices and 178,912 triangles. At eight tiers it
grows to 345 routes, 641 unique edges, about 160,002 vertices and 274,192
triangles. It remains one draw object, but it is already far larger than the
monumental trunk around it.

Inside the existing routed-light geometry, every packet uses the same narrow
centreline and the same texture row. That traffic has not been mapped onto C1's
later attachment-family carriers; it has no angular lane or chamber. Coincident
signals are maximum-composited for display. That operation discards visual
separability but does not merge the packet records.

## Why `GreaterDepth` is not physical containment

The core material is additive, double-sided, depth-tested with
`THREE.GreaterDepth`, does not write depth and is drawn after the opaque Tree.
A core fragment therefore appears when its depth is greater than the depth
already stored at that screen pixel.

This has four consequences:

1. it cannot distinguish the trunk from an unrelated foreground branch;
2. it suppresses a genuine core fragment where no shell fragment wrote depth,
   including an open seam or physical aperture;
3. it measures neither front/back shell intersections nor graphite thickness;
4. orbiting changes the screen-space mask rather than revealing stable radial
   placement, so the trunk route reads on the camera-facing surface.

This is undesirable view dependence. A real aperture would also be
view-dependent, but in the physically useful sense: its liner would show
parallax, solid sides would occlude the interior, and the contents would
disappear when the opening faces away.

## Connectivity, transport and transformation

| Mechanism | Present state | What Experiment C must not infer |
|---|---|---|
| Connectivity | Explicit nodes and child-to-parent edges | Proximity or visual overlap is not a connection. |
| Transport | Independent deterministic packet records move along exact routes | Sharing one atlas row is not aggregation. |
| Transformation | Not implemented | A colour change, wider wake or saturated trunk is not a transformed event. |

A physically meaningful interior can preserve this separation. A structural
carrier may be shared by many packets while each event remains independently
owned. One auditable event-sourcing hypothesis would give transformation
explicit arrival, consumption and output records at a named authored node; it
is not the only possible transformation model.

The collector-bundle hypothesis gives a useful bounded scale. With the default

- four in `crown-trunk-2 → crown-trunk-1`;
- six in `crown-trunk-1 → meaning`; and

C1 subsequently built the collector-bundle hypothesis as one neutral merged

## C1-inventory visibility approaches — pre-C2 hypotheses, now artistically rejected

This section preserves the rationale that led to C2. The bounded candidates were
worth testing and supplied useful technical evidence, but the final C2 review
rejects audience-facing disclosure of the carrier inventory as its artistic
premise. It does not reject every visible transducer. The table is historical
evidence, not a menu of current recommendations.

| Approach | Credibility and benefit | Risk / decision |
|---|---|---|
| **One authored aperture with a recessed inner liner** | A real opening gives parallax and self-occlusion while leaving most graphite opaque. It can be generated directly from the loft's station/ring structure without whole-Tree meshing. | Strongest physical candidate. It may resemble a service hatch, cut pipe or rune; it must be short, asymmetric and avoid the contour in the signed-off distant presets. |
| **Trunk-only front/back depth attenuation** | Separate trunk depths could prove that a core fragment lies between the trunk faces and vary disclosure with travel through the graphite body. It preserves the unbroken silhouette. | Still transillumination through nominally opaque graphite. It needs extra passes/render targets and may become a uniform glow at 11 packets/s. |
| **Small object-space thinning or transmissive inserts** | Local attributes could create mineral-like windows without changing the full shell. | Alpha and transmission can flatten wall thickness, introduce sorting problems and push the work toward a glass pipe. |
| **Trunk stencil mask** | Excludes disclosure outside the projected trunk silhouette and provides a useful diagnostic baseline. | A branch overlapping that silhouette can still supply the shared depth that passes `GreaterDepth`; stencil alone does not identify the occluder. It remains a screen-space projection, not a final artistic answer. |
| Global transparency, wireframe or emissive surface veins | Makes contents easy to see. | Destroys monumental mass or returns to decorative surface light. Reject. |
| SDF, raymarching or broad CSG reconstruction | Could model cavities and absorption in one system. | Duplicates the accepted loft, adds high complexity and approaches the rejected Gate 2 direction. Defer. |

The physical aperture could not use the trunk's `GreaterDepth` core because the
hole supplied no shell depth. C2 therefore rendered its trunk-local interior with
ordinary depth and allowed the remaining opaque shell to provide natural
occlusion.

The front/back candidate was deliberately bounded to a local region where a
camera ray crossed one contiguous trunk interval. Its depth was linearised and
described as a body chord rather than wall thickness because no inner shell
boundary existed.

C2 also confirmed that physical disclosure changed correctly under orbit while
the distant Silhouette and Canopy views retained mass. Those results remain
useful technical provenance. Close inspection of the C1 inventory is now reserved
for the opt-in engineering diagnostic. The separate C4a question is whether one
visible artwork-within-artwork transducer can preserve mystery without disclosing
that inventory.

## Transport confluence

At the accepted three-tier state, all 213 routes share
`meaning → exchange → uptake → planting-root`. The upper trunk is progressively
shared by 48, 32 and 16 routes at `crown-trunk-1`, `-2` and `-3`.

`meaning` has four genuine authored inlets:

| Incoming child | Eligible terminals | Mature average arrival rate at 11/s |
|---|---:|---:|
| `operations` | 40 | 2.07/s |
| `finance` | 58 | 3.00/s |
| `resources` | 67 | 3.46/s |
| `crown-trunk-1` | 48 | 2.48/s |

The upper-trunk inlet is structurally genuine but semantically mixed: its later
branch tiers contain Operations, Finance and Resources material. `meaning` can
therefore support a truthful structural-convergence experiment, but it cannot
yet claim to produce resolved business meaning.

The proposed provenance-led event transformation would require:

1. detecting each exact arrival and its incoming child edge;
2. retaining the source in a node-owned ledger or queue;
3. applying one inspectable node rule;
4. retiring each source exactly once; and
5. emitting a new event with a new identity and immutable provenance references.

A later candidate is an authored-inlet barrier with full flush: keep one FIFO
for each manifested direct child of `meaning`; once every required inlet has at
least one arrival, consume all waiting inputs and emit one provenance-linked
output. Consuming only one packet from each inlet would permanently accumulate
the unequal excess traffic. The full-flush rule avoids that particular leak, but
it remains a hypothesis and should follow an arrival-ledger and one-to-one
transduction proof. At the `0.20` default it would also be very slow to observe;
the signed-off `11` control is the appropriate experimental stress setting.
The required inlet set must be frozen after maturation, or the rule must define
reset/pass-through behaviour when manifestation changes; a dormant required
inlet must not be allowed to stall the other queues indefinitely.

## Artistic and technical risks recorded before C2

The aperture-, liner- and index-specific risks below are retained as historical
evidence and retired with the carrier-inventory disclosure direction.
High-frequency load, geometry cost and semantic overclaim remain relevant to the
visible filigree transducer and later transport work.

- **Loss of mass:** too many windows, broad transmission or saturated internal
  light would turn the trunk into a transparent pipe or circuit-board display.
- **False complexity:** filaments, repeated rings and glowing nodes can look
  intricate without corresponding to connectivity, load or transport.
- **Exposed construction:** an opening may reveal that lateral branches are
  intersecting tubes rather than manifold joins. Short inner branch-root
  continuations or port liners may eventually be required, without adding
  collars to the exterior.
- **Insufficient loft resolution:** 18 radial sides and ten samples per span are
  sufficient for the present silhouette but may not support a convincing liner
  or aperture boundary without local tessellation.
- **Growth mismatch:** the outer loft is rebuilt during maturation and all
  current materials share one deformation field. Liners and internal structures
  must not swim, crack or appear before their supporting span exists.
- **Index accounting:** the current growth draw range assumes every sidewall
  segment contributes a fixed `radialSegments × 6` indices. Holes and inner-wall
  faces require explicit span accounting rather than an incidental index order.
- **High-frequency washout:** at `11/0.50`, about 430 packet records can coexist,
  the shared lower trunk becomes nearly continuous light, and routed-light CPU
  work reaches about 8.5 ms per update before WebGL.
- **Geometry and draw cost:** the default artistic Tree already shows about 402
  meshes at maturity, rising to about 643 at eight tiers. New internals should be
  fixed and merged or instanced, never one mesh per packet or terminal.
- **Semantic overclaim:** a structurally authored confluence is not automatically
  a meaningful aggregation rule.

## C1 implementation record — approved

### Implemented boundary

C1 adds a deterministic attachment-family descriptor and one merged neutral
carrier skeleton. Each carrier is owned by one real lateral child of an active
trunk node, occupies every rootward span below that entry and retains a stable
normalised cross-sectional placement derived from entry direction and authored
descendant load. Deterministic collision relief is applied only where necessary;
there is no concentric slot lattice, ring decoration or helical winding.

The diagnostic is opt-in through **Isolate trunk carriers**. It removes the
engineered exterior and routed core from the inspection view, leaves the carrier
skeleton under the same deformation field, and restores the previous exterior
state on exit. It does not provide a technique for seeing the skeleton through
the opaque trunk.

No exterior loft, branch geometry, graphite material, packet route, packet
identity, schedule, traffic atlas, speed/frequency control, growth cycle,
`GreaterDepth` behaviour or topology was changed. No aperture, transparency,
chamber, packet remapping, transformation ledger or aggregation rule was added.

## C2 artistic review and redirection — 2026-10-11

### Review decision

C2 is technically successful and artistically rejected. Human review selected
none of the aperture, attenuation or stencil candidates. The failure is not a
lack of parallax, ownership masking or render quality; it is the proposition
that the Tree should disclose its hidden architecture as a countable inventory.

The finite Tree is intended to suggest potentially infinite recursive order.
What remains unseen contributes to that totality. By making the internal carriers
identifiable, every C2 candidate narrowed the imaginative field and diminished
the trunk's monumental presence. The aperture was the clearest example, but a
more organic hole or more polished attenuation would not resolve that inventory
problem. The result does not prohibit a singular visible transducer whose form is
itself an artwork and whose mechanism does not enumerate C1.

The accepted conclusion is therefore:

- preserve the intact graphite exterior and accepted silhouette as the baseline;
- retain C1 as hidden structural and causal truth, plus an opt-in engineering
  diagnostic;
- retain the C2 implementation as default-off experimental evidence pending a
  later cleanup decision, but do not refine or select it as an artistic language;
- reject visible carrier inventory and mechanical cutaway, not visibility itself;
- permit one visible artwork-within-artwork transducer whose traffic response is
  causally exact and whose larger mechanism remains unseen.

### Bounded technique

C3a implements one surface consequence of existing packet passage. It defines

crossings. Each site contains one small broken mark that writes and erodes in the

The mesh is fixed in trunk object space and sampled from the accepted graphite
loft. It uses the same relief and sway deformation, ordinary depth testing and
normal alpha blending. It adds no bloom, idle engraving, repeated band, aperture,

the rune layer out of the artwork view. The existing exterior, canopy, growth,
scheduler, authored routes, speed/frequency controls and hidden C1 descriptor are

### Causal and audit boundary

The runtime compares each existing packet's travelled route distance with the
three exact authored crossings. A rune record contains the packet, terminal,
parent/child span, crossing distance and crossing time. The packet is not delayed,
consumed, merged or given a new identity. Runtime diagnostics therefore state
`semanticClaim: passage` and `aggregation: false`.

At rates through `1` packet per second, every packet is admitted to the visual
layer. Above that rate, a stable hash of the packet event identity admits a
`1 / frequency` subset, holding the expected visible packet rate near one per
second. One admission decision follows the same causal packet through all three
sites; it does not align independent inputs. Cumulative crossing, admitted and
sampled-out counts remain exact. The recent immutable record ledger is bounded
to `4,096` entries and reports truncation, while active manifestations always
retain their causal packet IDs. This prevents an indefinitely running
`11`-packets-per-second study from accumulating diagnostic history without bound.
