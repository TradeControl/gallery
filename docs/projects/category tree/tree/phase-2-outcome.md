# Tree Phase 2 outcome — material character and restrained life

## Status

Implemented as a first material study for visual review, then amended after the first review proved too botanical.

The active experiment remains:

`/src/category-tree/tree/index.html`

## Purpose

Phase 1 proved that an explicit hierarchy could produce a recognisable structure and recursively unfold. It still looked like a constructed model because each connection was an independent grey tube with an exposed intersection.

Phase 2 therefore holds the complete 90-node topology stable and asks a narrower question: can the same finite hierarchy be perceived as one beautiful, engineered and responsive instrument?

The first reconstruction over-weighted biological age. Its coarse implicit sampling, green-grey palette and strong relief made the result resemble a gnarled tree covered in organic growth. That was a conceptual failure even though the surface was continuous. The governing image has therefore been corrected to: **a windswept, recursive, engineered organism—not a tree, but tree-like.**

## Reconstruction decision

An implicit scalar-field reconstruction was implemented first. It successfully fused intersections, but its additive volume and coarse sampling created swollen, irregular branches. Raising resolution and replacing additive metaballs with capsule-distance fields improved continuity without correcting the governing biological character. The mechanism was therefore rejected as the default artistic surface for this phase rather than polished into a better imitation of bark.

The amended Tree uses precision-cast swept conduits along the same finite cubic curves used by the topology diagnostic. Their radii continue to derive from node roles and conduit weight. A small number of shared bronze, graphite and pale mineral-metal materials distinguishes structural scale without displaying category colours.

This changes the rendering mechanism without changing the hierarchy:

- 90 explicit nodes;
- 89 explicit parent-child connections;
- one root;
- 56 terminals;
- maximum depth 8.

No junction spheres, beads or node ornaments are present in the artistic state. Topology nodes and the original role-coloured conduits remain independently available as diagnostic overlays but are hidden by default. The unused implicit-field module remains in the experiment as evidence of the rejected approach.

## Material language

The amended surface combines three restrained associations:

- mineral presence in its monumental weight and warm restrained palette;
- precision casting in its smooth dark-metal response and deliberate sections;
- fibre only as very shallow directional relief subordinate to the overall form.

The green-grey cast has been removed. Role-dependent colours now stay within bronze, warm graphite and pale mineral-metal values. They provide gradual material variation rather than turning the Tree into a colour-coded diagram. There are no luminous points or decorative pulses.

A new wind-sweep parameter progressively displaces later generations while preserving every explicit relationship. It gives the recursion a common directional pressure and weakens the bilateral biological-tree silhouette without flattening the crown into a wall.

Warm key light and cool edge light expose volume. A neutral-background switch separates material and silhouette inspection from the darker intended atmosphere.

## Restrained response

The Tree has a very slow, low-amplitude redistribution across its horizontal structure. This is shader deformation of the already proven conduits, not arbitrary branch motion or new topology. It can be frozen completely. Surface relief and response amplitude are independently adjustable.

## Controls and diagnostics

The Phase 2 page provides:

- crown spread;
- vertical rise;
- asymmetry;
- conduit weight;
- wind sweep;
- surface relief;
- structural response;
- freeze response;
- dark or neutral background;
- engineered-body visibility;
- topology nodes, finite connections, role colours and local axes;
- silhouette, canopy and structure cameras.

The Phase 1b growth-cycle module and explicit latent fixture remain in the codebase, but unfolding is intentionally not active in this material-isolation pass. Recombining growth with the engineered body should happen only if this direction survives visual review.

## Verification

- The active hierarchy validates successfully.
- The engineered body follows the diagnostic topology and occupies the same world-space frame.
- Junction ornaments are absent in the default view.
- Neutral and dark inspection backgrounds both render correctly.
- Motion can be frozen without changing structural meaning.
- No new activity, glow, information packets, molten transport or Casting Pit integration has been introduced.

## Assessment boundary

The amended pass removes the gnarled botanical treatment, visible junction ornaments and green-grey cast while introducing directional sweep. It is cleaner and more deliberately engineered, but it does not claim to have found the final beautiful silhouette. The crown can still read as sparse or antler-like from some views, and sharp conduit meetings remain a technical boundary of this non-implicit construction.

Visual review should distinguish these questions:

1. Does the precision-cast conduit language move the work far enough away from a biological tree?
2. Is the mineral/cast/fibrous material language promising?
3. Should later crown work make the Tree denser and more vertically distributed without changing its explicit topology contract?

Phase 2 should not be considered signed off merely because the biological material error has been removed.
