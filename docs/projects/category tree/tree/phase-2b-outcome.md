# Tree Phase 2b outcome — dynamic chromatic emergence

## Status

Implemented for visual review.

## Governing principle

> The structure should be materially dark and only partially knowable; dynamic colour should continually disclose its recursion.

This principle is taken from the artistic success of the archived prototype, not from its geometry. The prototype's radial arms, absent functional trunk, coordinate-folded pseudo-topology and arbitrary glow are not reproduced.

## Implementation

The validated 90-node engineered Tree remains the structural source. Its passive material has been darkened to near-black graphite and bronze. Three connected principal limbs receive different slow phases, while relationship depth delays illumination across generations. Fine spatial variation produces local constellations inside each broader manifestation.

The active palette is deliberately distinct from the Casting Pit:

- cyan and cold white dominate;
- electric blue provides depth;
- warm gold appears less frequently as a resolving accent;
- the passive body remains materially dark.

The initial trunk treatment used an additional three-lane helical manifestation. Visual review correctly identified this as an overly literal, barber-pole interpretation of the prototype. It is superseded by the irregular longitudinal transmission introduced in Phase 2c.

## Controls

- Emergence intensity can reduce the Tree to its passive engineered body or push the light into a stronger manifestation.
- Emergence speed changes the slow material cycle.
- Gold presence controls how often the cool field resolves into a warm accent.
- Freeze response stops structural deformation but does not currently freeze the chromatic cycle.

## Conceptual boundary

This is dynamic material manifestation, not information traffic. No event is emitted by a terminal, no packet owns an edge, and no aggregation occurs at a confluence. Those remain Phase 3 concerns.

The illumination is topology-aware at the level of principal limb and relationship generation. It avoids the prototype's radial starburst and does not create branches that are absent from the explicit model.

## Review question

Does partial, moving illumination allow the engineered topology to acquire mystery and beauty without surrendering its trunk, finite relationships or non-botanical character?
