# Tree

## Concept

The Tree is a monumental recursive instrument: an engineered organism that receives many terminal activities and composes them into higher-order meaning. Its governing visual proposition is **a windswept, recursive, engineered organism—not a tree, but tree-like**. Biological resemblance is a structural analogy, not an invitation to bark, foliage, slime or naturalistic tree simulation; beauty, logic and deliberate construction come first.

It should combine the presence of an ancient tree, the purpose of a refinery, the responsiveness of a nervous system and the precision of a computational structure. Stone, cast metal, glass, fibre, circuitry and living tissue are possible material associations, but none should dominate so completely that the object becomes merely botanical, mechanical or digital.

Most importantly, the Tree must branch because it has a topology. It is built from finite nodes and connections with actual parent-child relationships. Recursive form emerges from that structure; it is not simulated by repeatedly folding space or decorating a trunk with procedural filaments.

## Structural language

Terminal elements should be distinct from intermediate categories and larger confluences. Connections should have readable beginnings and ends. Junctions should feel capable of receiving, transforming and forwarding activity. Variation in scale, spacing, rhythm and material can communicate different roles without reducing the artwork to a labelled diagram.

The structure need not resemble a conventional upright tree. It may be an arbor, suspended instrument, branching foundry or asymmetrical recursive architecture. It may contain dormant regions, disconnected structures or transverse devices that read across several branches. Whatever form it takes, a viewer should be able to sense that many specific things become fewer, larger meanings through a sequence of relationships.

The Tree as a whole is the transducer. The trunk is a principal artistic and functional element, not a machine whose internal anatomy must be displayed. Information descends through the recursive canopy and, in the complete work, acquires higher-order meaning through truthful aggregation. The monumental graphite trunk carries that ordered passage towards Cosmos. Visibility is not forbidden: one exceptional, causally active transducer may be visible as an artwork within the artwork without turning the Tree into an exposed inventory of parts.

The rejected C3a study demonstrated that a packet can truthfully trigger a temporary surface mark, but a passive indicator is not a functional rune. A first C4a surface-maze candidate then exposed an ontological break: the visible rune sat on the graphite while the accepted transport ran through the trunk's centre. Hidden radial joins made the coordinates continuous but did not make that visual sentence convincing. A subsequent wholly internal, depth-disclosed labyrinth remains useful as a technical prototype for coordinate-continuous transport, but it is superseded as the artistic candidate: subsurface visibility alone does not answer what the transducer should be.

The revised proposition is one intricate, three-dimensional gold filigree transducer at the `meaning` passage. Its point of departure is the artist's memory of complex African gold filigree droplet earrings worn in the 1980s—not a claim to a generic “African style,” nor permission to copy an unidentified cultural motif. That memory contributes gold, intricacy and volumetric delicacy, but not the placement logic of jewellery attached to a surface. The transducer should be a longitudinal, DNA-like volume within the trunk: camera-independent, organised around the trunk axis and visibly received by the graphite rather than staged on one face. A cyan source must visibly enter the same object that transforms it; it is consumed exactly once at an authored boundary, and one distinct Cosmos-gold output leaves with immutable single-source provenance. It must not become a painted rune, mechanical cutaway, permanent display of C1 carriers or decorative jewel unaffected by traffic. This remains one-to-one transduction with `aggregation: false`, not higher-order synthesis. Gold is a visual vocabulary borrowed from Cosmos, not evidence that the Tree has been integrated with Cosmic circuitry.

The filigree direction is now closed as an unresolved experiment rather than an accepted Tree language. **C4a-E1 — exterior filigree evidence**, immutable profile `c4a-exterior-brooch-v1`, proved coordinate-continuous traversal, causal cyan-to-gold light, ordinary-depth occlusion and exact one-to-one provenance, but its compact exterior loops read as a brooch attached to the trunk. **C4a-E2 — longitudinal placement/presentation evidence**, profile `c4a-longitudinal-placement-v1`, proved that one causal rail can be buried through most of the graphite and revealed at three separated axial crossings. Its last green implementation remains reproducible by explicit profile selection as evidence, but it is not an artistic solution: the sparse crossings do not disclose a convincing DNA-like volume, the selected rail favours one Structure-facing flank, and its safety-driven phase can change when the Tree's form controls rebuild the trunk. A later fixed, circumferential trial removed that visual snap but collided with hidden C1 carriers across much of the supported control envelope; that incomplete trial was reverted. Runtime evidence continues to state `spatialConvergenceClaim: false` and `aggregation: false`, with one provenance-linked gold identity for each admitted cyan identity. No filigree candidate is signed off and genuine many-to-one transformation remains reserved for C5.

**C4b-O1 — exterior optical-fibre spiral** is a separately authorised and
strictly bounded experiment rather than a continuation of the filigree studies.
It asks whether one narrow, continuous route can descend from the canopy-side
`meaning` region across the monumental trunk's successive spans and resume
rootward near its base. The route lies just outside the intact graphite shell,
follows its taper and movement in Tree object space, and accepts ordinary scene
depth so that the trunk genuinely hides its rearward turns under orbit. It should
be materially almost absent at rest: real packet passage alone discloses a local
length of fibre. Existing blue packet identities enter at the upper end and move
independently down the same spiral while their visible colour changes gradually
towards the gold vocabulary of Cosmos near the rootward resume. That colour
progression describes passage through the bounded route; it does not prove
many-to-one transformation or higher-order meaning. The existing one-to-one
causal ledger remains authoritative, with immutable single-source provenance and
`aggregation: false`. One candidate is now implemented and technically verified;
it awaits human artistic review without further refinement. This authorisation
covers that candidate and its review only. It does not authorise C5, aggregation,
root construction, Casting Pit
integration, Cosmos transport or any redesign of the Tree.

Eventually mycelium-like roots will enter the Casting Pit gravity well. Information will continue downward through those roots into Cosmic circuitry while molten material from the physical Universe moves upward through a distinct transport to sustain the Tree. These opposed processes are causally distinct but compositionally connected. Neither may be reduced to decorative animation, and neither requires the internal C1 architecture to become audience-facing imagery.

## Behaviour

Animation should reveal the Tree's operation. In the eventual work, small packet events can enter at terminals, travel through conduits and acquire higher-order meaning only where an explicit aggregation rule genuinely consumes and transforms multiple independent inputs. A changed colour, surface mark, shared channel or visual convergence is not proof of that transformation. Most of the current routed stream still establishes information passage only. C4a claims no more than its hidden ledger can prove: one source is consumed and one provenance-linked output is created. C3a's passive marks cannot be read as transduction, and neither C4a-E1 nor the active E2 placement study establishes aggregation or higher-order meaning.

C4b-O1 applies the same discipline to an exterior route. Its gradual blue-to-gold
colour is a visible account of where one independent packet is within the
descent, not evidence that several inputs have become one output. Between real
events the fibre should withdraw from attention rather than becoming a permanent
decorative cable.

Opposed kinds of flow may retain distinct directions, lanes, colours or rhythms. A selected interpretation may illuminate one meaningful route while leaving the rest present but subdued. A disabled branch may darken recursively; a disconnected branch may remain viable outside the main structure; a changed relationship may visibly reroute subsequent flow.

The Tree should also reveal its own continuing formation. It begins with an established, recognisable structural scaffold—trunk, principal limbs and the initial 36-node hierarchy—not with a bare trunk or a replay of germination. Additional explicit category and terminal subtrees then unfold recursively across that existing crown until it reaches a visible viability threshold. This evolution is neither arbitrary procedural decoration nor ambient sway. It is the temporal manifestation of additional topology, with reproducible but unequal growth rhythms. Once viable, the Tree remains responsive rather than becoming a frozen still object.

These behaviours offer an artistic vocabulary for classification, aggregation, polarity, selected roots, enablement, disconnection, reclassification and expressions. They should be introduced selectively. The Tree should first establish structural truth and visual presence before it attempts to portray the full conceptual range of Trade Control.

## Intended experience

From a distance, the Tree should read as one extraordinary object with a strong silhouette and monumental balance. At closer range, its hierarchy should become discoverable: terminals, finite conduits, junctions, repeated motifs and traces of activity. Complexity should reward inspection without collapsing into noise.

The finite visible form should imply a potentially infinite recursive order. Omission and unseen depth are active parts of the work. C1's carrier architecture remains hidden causal structure and an opt-in diagnostic facility; C2's aperture, attenuation and stencil disclosures remain rejected because they exposed a countable inventory. That decision does not ban all visibility. A single visible filigree transducer may intensify rather than diminish mystery if its axial volume feels held within the monumental trunk rather than attached to its surface, while the larger mechanism remains unseen.

The C4b-O1 question is narrower: can transient packets momentarily reveal a
three-dimensional descending spiral without turning the monumental graphite body
into either an exposed mechanism or a decorated pipe? Its success depends on
causal light, real occlusion and restraint, not on the fibre being continuously
legible.

The structure should be materially dark and only partially knowable; dynamic colour should continually disclose its recursion. Illumination is therefore capable of manifesting broad connected regions and successive generations without pretending to be an information packet. The Tree should not need uniform visibility to prove that it exists.

The object may move, but motion should preserve structural legibility. Slow organic response, loading, tension or minute adjustment can make the Tree feel alive; indiscriminate sway and flicker should not obscure its relationships. Light and colour should clarify stable differences or active events rather than provide interchangeable atmosphere.

The Tree will first be judged in a neutral environment. This protects the construction problem from the later compositional problem of making it emerge from the completed Casting Pit and its converging molten flows.

## Implementation awareness

An explicit hierarchy held outside the shader is the natural source of truth for nodes and edges. Rendering may combine ordinary geometry, instancing, shader deformation and procedural surface detail, but those techniques should express a known recursive structure. A small representative hierarchy is enough for early studies; connection to real Category Tree data is a later possibility, not a prerequisite for artistic success.

Live controls should focus on meaningful dimensions such as hierarchy depth, branching density, sibling spacing, conduit thickness, terminal and node scale, asymmetry, organic-to-engineered balance, motion, flow and inspection views. Presets may eventually isolate structure, polarity, aggregation or disconnected forms.

## Provenance

The archived Category Tree shader established valuable mood: a dark engineered organism, mineral surfaces, molten and circuit-like energy, ceremonial motion and industrial scale. Its assessment also identified the decisive limitation: repeated coordinate folding produced branch-like decoration rather than a heterogeneous recursive topology. Glowing bands were not nodes, tubes were not clearly finite relationships, and motion did not demonstrate classification or aggregation.

The archive is therefore a record of visual instincts and failure modes, not the design of the new Tree. The new work should retain the ambition and atmosphere while allowing structure and behaviour to generate the image.
