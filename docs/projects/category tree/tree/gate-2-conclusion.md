# Gate 2 conclusion — continuous-surface experiment

Gate 2 investigated whether the Category Tree should become one physically
continuous surface. The experiment demonstrated that strong manifold topology
and recoverable hierarchy provenance could be achieved for substantial recursive
Tree states. It also established useful audit, worker and meshing techniques.

The resulting geometry nevertheless damaged the signed-off visual form. Junction
swelling, collars, sockets, wedges and related meshing deformation replaced the
clean engineered character of the Tree. Physical surface continuity was therefore
abandoned as an artistic requirement.

The experiment is preserved on branch
`archive/gate-2-continuous-surface` at commit `ffe6004`.

## The Transducer

Gate 2 clarified the Tree's role as **The Transducer**. Its connectivity is
semantic and dynamic rather than dependent on a single manifold skin:

- matter and energy will flow outward and upward from the gravity well through
  the trunk and hierarchy;
- information will flow inward and downward through authored hierarchy paths;
- information will converge on the trunk, where later techno-runes may
  participate in transformation or encoding; and
- transformed information will enter the communications of the Cosmos.

This conclusion records direction only. No Transducer behaviour, luminosity,
information packets, techno-runes, matter flow or Cosmos communication is
implemented here.
