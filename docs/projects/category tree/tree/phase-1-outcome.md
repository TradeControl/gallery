# Tree Phase 1 — recursive form outcome

**Date:** 27 September 2026  
**Status:** continuing; static form established but recursive emergence still required

## Principal question

Can the explicit Phase 0 hierarchy become a recognisable, volumetric Tree with a sustained trunk and monumental silhouette without losing its finite parent-child relationships?

The experiment remains at:

`src/category-tree/tree/index.html`

When Vite is running, the usual URL is:

## Preserved structural truth

Phase 1 does not change the representative hierarchy:

- 36 explicit nodes;
- 35 explicit parent-child relationships;
- one planting root;
- three successive trunk stages;
- three principal crown limbs;
- 20 terminals;
- maximum relationship depth 7.

Every visible conduit is still generated from one model edge. No decorative branch has been added, and no shader repetition creates apparent recursion.

## Volumetric recursive layout

The radial crown introduced at the end of Phase 0 remains the basis of the form. Each subtree receives an angular territory proportional to its terminal population. Descendants spread outward and upward within that territory.

The layout now exposes four artistic dimensions:

- **Crown spread** changes horizontal reach without changing node or edge count.
- **Vertical rise** changes trunk and branch ascent without changing topology.
- **Asymmetry** scales deterministic variation in angular placement, reach, rise and conduit curvature.
- **Conduit weight** scales the complete tapered structural network.

All variations remain deterministic. Reset restores the reviewed defaults rather than neutral mathematical values.

## Curved tapered conduits

Straight diagnostic cylinders have been replaced by finite cubic curves. Each curve begins and ends at the positions owned by its parent and child nodes.

A custom tube builder samples each curve and constructs a radial mesh whose radius blends from a role-dependent parent radius to a smaller child radius. This creates structural taper through:

- root;
- trunk;
- totals;
- categories;
- terminals.

The conduit ends remain topologically exact even when the visible route bends. Curvature is derived from stable node identifiers and the live asymmetry setting.

## Trunk and junction load

The trunk is substantially heavier than subsidiary relationships and tapers towards the crown. It remains one explicit root-to-crown route rather than a cylinder placed behind the hierarchy.

Junction meshes cover conduit meetings and scale by both node role and the number of terminals beneath the node. Higher confluences therefore carry visibly more mass than terminal endpoints.

Topology-node meshes and their role colours are now hidden by default. They remain available through the **Topology nodes** diagnostic switch, with role colouring controlled independently. The artistic state contains no deliberate node markers.

## Camera and inspection

The three camera states now serve different Phase 1 questions:

- **Silhouette** judges the complete upright encounter.
- **Canopy** checks radial occupation and exposes any return to a wall-trained layout.
- **Structure** approaches the crown closely enough to inspect taper and junction construction.

Topology nodes, finite connections, role colours and local planting axes remain independently switchable.

## Visual findings

The Silhouette view now reads as a trunk supporting branching limbs rather than a graph drawn with rods. Taper gives the common route visible load, and the stronger curves produce an upward sweep without changing the hierarchy.

The Canopy view continues to show three separated angular territories with substantial extent on both horizontal axes. Branches hide and reveal one another as the camera changes, confirming that the crown is volumetric.

The Structure view exposes the present boundary of the study. With topology nodes hidden, there are no deliberate beads or junction ornaments. Live inspection nevertheless shows seams and angular unions where the current independent tube meshes meet. The artistic requirement is therefore not yet met: hiding the model nodes is necessary, but not sufficient. A unified implicit branch field, or an equivalently continuous surface reconstruction, is the preferred next experiment because it can fuse forks while also supporting progressive tip growth. The separate finite conduits can remain as diagnostics. The sparse representative hierarchy can also read as antler-like from some angles. These are legitimate Phase 1 review questions, not problems to conceal with material texture or glow.

More importantly, the present Tree remains unchanged through time. Its form can be remixed, but it does not yet develop additional complexity. The current 36-node form should remain fully manifested at page load as a recognisable starting scaffold; the missing behaviour is recursive elaboration across that existing crown, not root-first germination.

The object remains deliberately untextured. It has neutral structural materials, simple lighting and no animation. Phase 2 material work has not been pulled forward to manufacture presence.

## Transport direction retained

No activity has been implemented. The agreed future transport laws remain:

- molten physical material rises from the buried root;
- terminal information aggregates inward through the canopy;
- information packets travel down the trunk into Cosmos.

The Phase 1 geometry establishes one shared trunk capable of carrying those visibly opposed transports later. It does not simulate them yet.

## Recommendation

Continue with Phase 1b before Phase 2.

The key questions are:

1. Does the new silhouette possess enough structural promise to continue?
2. Does the radial crown feel like an emerging tree rather than an antler or abstract network?
3. Do the branch unions remain visually continuous with topology diagnostics disabled?
4. Does the trunk have sufficient weight to become the future lava/information transducer?

The next experiment should retain the complete 36-node scaffold, add deterministic latent category/terminal subtrees at eligible crown sites, and progressively manifest those additions until an inspectable viability condition is reached. It should not spend time displaying a bare trunk or pollarded crown. Information packets, molten uptake, material emission and atmospheric effects remain withheld.
