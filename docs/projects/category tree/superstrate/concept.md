# Superstrate

## Concept

The superstrate is metaphysical emergence made visible. It occupies the apparent upper world but is not a sky, nebula, atmosphere or astronomical backdrop. It contains no stars or celestial bodies. It is ghostly without being a ghost: a field whose presence seems to arise from the other systems in the artwork rather than scenery placed behind them.

Its purpose is to make the formerly empty upper space consequential. The completed work extends below the terrain through the substrate and above the terrain through the superstrate, so the Category Tree appears to inhabit a continuous causal world rather than standing before a black screen.

## Four localized influences

The superstrate manifests four causes. Each has its own direction, angular reach, procedural morphology, phase and motion:

- **Substrate** contributes low-frequency depth pressure.
- **Physical Universe** contributes a restrained warm condensation associated with molten matter and the Casting Pit.
- **Information/Cosmos** contributes pearlescent generative order.
- **Living/Tree** contributes a recursively warped reach.

These are localized fields of force or influence, not uniform colour controls. Increasing one magnet strengthens manifestation around its corresponding source; it does not wash the entire shell equally. Their unequal placement and movement create the complexity of the field.

## Confluence

Confluence is a fifth visible field but not a fifth source. It is derived from the six pairwise overlaps of the four causal influences. It therefore cannot manifest when only one source is active.

Artistically, Confluence is relation becoming perceptible. It is the part of the superstrate that belongs to no component in isolation: an emergent whole produced by their coexistence. Its control governs how strongly those interactions become visible without inventing another independent cause.

## Motion and manifestation

The superstrate cannot be static. Each causal field advects and pulses at a different incommensurate rate, so their relationship continually changes without settling into one obvious loop. **Presence** governs overall manifestation, **Coherence** governs how legibly the field organizes, and **Breath** amplifies local pulsation without being the source of motion. Only Freeze stops the field.

The rendering is an observer-relative directional shell. Translation has no parallax, while rotation and movement can disclose a different portion of the field. This helps it remain metaphysical: it surrounds perception without becoming a distant physical object.

## Relation to the composition

The completed composition provides four real world anchors: a point on the substrate surface, a physical point on the Casting Pit membrane, an information-region point and the living Tree crown. The composition adapter continually resolves these positions into directions from the active camera and updates the shared superstrate engine.

This is a genuine connection to the other layers. The sources are neither decorative labels nor four uniform sliders. At the same time, the relationship is deliberately one-way: the superstrate manifests their influence but does not feed numerical forces back into the substrate, molten transport, Cosmos or Tree. It is an artistic account of metaphysical consequence, not a physical simulation claim.

Diagnostic views may isolate each influence and Confluence so their individual contribution can be understood. Those modes are development instruments rather than separate finished skies.

## Boundary and provenance

The superstrate must remain caused by the artwork rather than resemble astronomical wallpaper. It owns no stars, planets, textures, scene lights or lower-layer geometry. It does not import or inspect the engines whose influence it manifests.

The study began because the finished composition contained a large black absence above the world. A single pulsating nebula would have filled the space but contributed no causal meaning. Separating four localized influences and deriving Confluence from their interaction turned that absence into a field belonging specifically to Category Tree.
