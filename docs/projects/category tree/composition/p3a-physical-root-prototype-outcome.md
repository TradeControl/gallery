# Category Tree composition — P3a physical-root prototype outcome

**Implemented:** 2026-10-25
**Status:** technical evidence retained; active visual acceptance suspended after the 2026-10-25 regression review
**Scope:** give the accepted Tree a composition-owned collar and buried load path without changing the standalone Tree

## Proposition

The earlier planting point was spatially valid but visually incomplete. The standalone Tree's short planted extension supplied neither a root flare nor a larger buried extent, so translation and scale alone could not give the trunk convincing physical weight.

The composition now adds one derived physical system beneath the accepted Tree:

- an uncapped, `18`-sided collar loft begins at a real intact trunk shoulder and follows the measured P2 contact side-for-side;
- five asymmetrical principal roots carry the three authored `meaning` loads in counter-load directions and occupy the two largest remaining angular gaps;
- five finer continuations peel from those roots and end uncapped in the P2 deep-medium band;
- all geometry is generated in the live planting transform from the current trunk mesh, hierarchy, membrane and planting envelope.

This is designed to avoid a botanical starburst and does not reuse the internal gold router. The root axes retain hierarchy provenance, but the physical form remains dark, faceted and structural.

## Boundary ownership

The root generator submits actual surface vertices—not merely centre-lines—to the P2 contract. Every principal and fine-root surface remains below the live membrane. Shoulder geometry above the protected terrain-surface band is classified as collar and restricted to the measured trunk–terrain contact footprint; triangulated edges that remain inside that band are treated as terrain contact. The measured contact footprint is authoritative for above-surface collar containment; the planting-root footprint remains a construction and diagnostic boundary. Endpoint reach is reduced when necessary to retain meaningful Gaussian-well influence.

At the P3a review boundary, the physical terrain owned occlusion with ordinary depth testing and buried roots were not exposed in artwork mode. P7/F3 later removed that opaque basin presentation from the active composition; root placement still follows the mathematical membrane, and separate `Physical root guides` plus the optional membrane wireframe remain available for diagnosis. The detached Tree-local D4 ownership proxy prevents internal gold trails from showing through the well, but it does not provide ordinary scene-depth burial for the graphite physical-root mesh.

## Preservation

The prototype is a sibling of the accepted Tree inside the composition planting transform. It:

- owns its generated geometry and diagnostic resources;
- owns a material derived from the live monumental-trunk material, retaining its graphite response and shared deformation uniforms without owning or mutating the Tree material;
- owns a per-vertex sway handoff: weight `1` at the intact shoulder, interpolating to `0` at the buried collar ring below contact; every principal and fine-root vertex has zero sway weight while retaining the graphite relief response;
- never enters the Tree's cyan/gold internal render passes;
- does not mutate the source trunk geometry, draw range, hierarchy or standalone source;
- rebuilds from the new live trunk after a form change and follows scale, settlement, orientation and membrane changes.

The original `0.18`-unit extension remains untouched in the standalone Tree. In composition, the derived shoulder and flare visually supersede it without introducing a visible cap, rock, sleeve or contact ring.

## Verification evidence

- Default geometry contains one `108`-vertex collar with a split buried shading crease, five principal roots and five finer continuations: `2,118` vertices and `3,984` sidewall triangles, with no indexed end caps.
- Every principal and fine-root surface vertex remains below the membrane; only collar/shoulder geometry may rise through the measured contact region.
- All ten endpoints classify as `deep-medium`; default minimum endpoint influence is about `0.221` for principal roots and `0.156` for continuations.
- Scale / settlement corner sweeps at `0.90 / 0.00`, `0.90 / 1.00`, `1.30 / 0.00` and `1.30 / 1.00` remain attached, contained and deep-medium.
- The full inherited depression-width range (`1.5…6.0`) is covered. Root reach is solved against a preferred influence budget at normal widths and a contact-relative budget when a narrow well makes the preferred value physically impossible. A non-monotonic search checks the generated tube vertices and triangle probes along the whole path, so a root cannot cross a low-influence trough merely because its endpoint later recovers.
- Live Tree-only, well/root, contact and subterrain inspection confirmed a continuous flared base, terrain-owned burial and a diagnostic that remains depth-tested.
- The complete Category Tree suite passes: `103` tests, `0` failures.
- The production composition builds successfully. Vite retains its pre-existing advisory that the main bundle exceeds `500 kB`.

## Review boundary

P3a supplies a technically coherent physical planting proposition; it is not yet an accepted final sculpture. In particular, the number, breadth and visibility of the root lobes are artistic decisions for review.

The collar is a visually continuous overlapping surface, not yet a welded, watertight lower-trunk occupancy. The original buried base cap remains enclosed. P4 must therefore use an explicit analytical occupancy boundary or a later welded composition clone rather than treating this presentation mesh as a collision solid.

No molten collision, lava meniscus, dark-matter substrate or Cosmos restyling is introduced here. P3 adds only the composition-local shoulder-to-static-root sway handoff, not a broader crown-motion redesign. P4 should not bind live molten behaviour to this collar until the P3 shape is accepted or revised.

That correction does not close P3. The physical-root calculations still derive from the live planting transform, mathematical membrane and envelope, and the automated scale, settlement, orientation and width sweeps remain valid. However, removing the opaque terrain also removed ordinary depth ownership for the dark buried root mesh. Active artistic acceptance is therefore suspended until the root and subterrain cameras confirm a deliberate burial solution that does not mask lava or Cosmos and does not reintroduce the brown basin as camouflage.
