# Category Tree — Monumental Trunk

## Context

Gate 2 is concluded and archived as a failed experiment. Do not resume it.

The Transducer concept is unchanged. Gate 2 was a failed attempt to materialise
its connectivity by making the complete Tree one continuous surface.

The restored graphite Tree is the accepted baseline. Preserve its crown,
hierarchy, recursive unfolding and existing behaviour.

## Task

The present trunk looks like an enlarged branch or sapling. It must become the
monumental load-bearing body of the Tree and the future physical transducer
between the Universe and Cosmos.

Implement one bounded trunk study.

The trunk should:

- read as one substantial engineered volume;
- have broad lower mass and slow taper towards the crown;
- reflect the unequal load of the crown through restrained asymmetry;
- integrate visually with the principal limbs without attempting to make the
  crown manifold;
- retain the dark glinting graphite material language;
- provide credible physical volume for later information and matter transport.

A continuous trunk is desirable because it is a single structural object, but
visual quality outranks manifold elegance.

Do not produce stacked cylinders, collars, sockets, bands, junction blobs,
naturalistic bark, roots, nodules or arbitrary organic noise.

Do not use luminosity to improve or conceal the geometry.

Inspect the static picture version `.local/category-tree.png` for inspiration.

## Protected baseline

Do not redesign the crown or change its hierarchy, layout, growth tiers,
recursive unfolding, branch geometry or accepted graphite treatment.

Do not revive the archived Gate 2 whole-tree meshing architecture.

## Working boundary

Inspect the existing trunk and choose the smallest credible dedicated-trunk
approach.

Implement **one approach only**.

Small corrections to make that approach work are permitted. If it exposes a
fundamental problem, stop and report it rather than trying another architecture.

Do not undertake broad parameter searches, expensive meshing experiments or
recursive investigation of newly discovered problems.

## Visual review

Judge the result in Silhouette, Canopy and Structure views.

It succeeds only if:

- the trunk no longer reads as a sapling;
- its mass is credible relative to the crown;
- taper is gradual;
- asymmetry feels load-bearing rather than noisy;
- the crown appears supported by it;
- the accepted crown remains substantially unchanged;
- recursive unfolding still works;
- no Gate 2-style swelling, collars, sockets or other deformation appears.

A technically continuous trunk that looks worse is a failure.

Create:

`docs/projects/category tree/tree/trunk-investigation.md`

Record briefly:

- the approach used;
- files changed;
- technical verification;
- visual strengths and defects;
- verdict: **CONTINUE / REVISE / ABANDON**;
- one recommended next block.

Do not implement the recommended next block.

Do not implement information packets, luminosity, techno-runes, lava,
Universe/Cosmos integration or other Transducer behaviour.

When this single trunk study has been implemented, inspected and documented:

**STOP and return control for visual review. Do not commit.**
