# Gallery — Category Tree: Artistic Direction and Next Experiment

## The larger conception

I want to clarify the artistic destination before we continue Experiment C.

**The Tree as a whole is the transducer.** The trunk is not a machine whose internal components must be displayed.

My conception is that information packets descend through the recursive canopy, acquiring higher-order meaning through aggregation. As they pass through the monumental trunk, restrained techno-runes express their translation towards the ordered Cosmos.

Eventually, mycelium-like roots will extend into the Casting Pit gravity well. Information will descend through those roots into the Cosmic circuitry, while molten material from the physical Universe moves in the opposite direction, supplying the Tree.

These are distinct but connected processes, not three decorative animations.

The Tree's finite form should suggest a potentially infinite recursive order. Like traditional Chinese landscape painting, what remains unseen is an essential part of its monumentality.

Do not expose the internal carrier architecture in the artwork. Retain C1 as a valid hidden mechanism and diagnostic facility. C2's visibility experiments remain rejected.

## Immediate objective — a visible trunk experiment

We've made considerable conceptual and technical progress, but very little artistic progress in the rendering itself.

I want the next bounded experiment to produce something visually inspectable rather than another extended design review.

Investigate **restrained techno-rune manifestations on the existing opaque graphite trunk**, driven by actual information-packet passage.

The runes should:

- appear only in response to genuine packet events;
- occupy meaningful positions along the existing trunk;
- briefly manifest and then disappear;
- suggest translation or transformation without depicting an exposed machine;
- preserve the monumental graphite mass and accepted silhouette;
- remain legible under orbit without becoming fixed decorative markings.

They must not imply that semantic aggregation has already occurred. At this stage they express information passage, not proven many-to-one transformation.

Keep the existing canopy, packet scheduler, authored routes, growth behaviour and C1 architecture unchanged.

Avoid repeating bands, arbitrary glyph patterns, broad surface illumination, bloom-dependent effects or anything resembling a circuit-board display.

Please update the Tree concept and Work Plan to record the clarified artistic direction and the distinction between information passage and genuine transduction.

You may choose the specific visual technique, provided it preserves the constraints above.

Run the technical checks, inspect the result in Silhouette, Structure and Canopy views, and record what works and what does not.
