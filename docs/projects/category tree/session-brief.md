# Gallery — Casting Pit v0.5a: Universe / Cosmos Overlay

## Purpose

This experiment combines two systems that have already been developed and judged independently:

- **Universe** — the molten physical system developed through Casting Pit v0.3 (`src/casting-pit/physical-layer`.
- **Cosmos** — the hierarchical ordered circuitry developed through v0.4b (`src/casting-pit/information-layer`).

This is the first experiment in which they inhabit the same scene.

Do not redesign either system merely because they are now being combined.

The central question is:

> **Can matter and information inhabit the same distorted reality while remaining visibly governed by different laws?**

Or, in the shorthand established during the previous work:

> **Matter falls. Information is routed.**

Read the existing Category Tree concept documents, the completed v0.3a material, `sessions/session-brief-10.md`, the current `v0.4b review.md`, the v0.4b outcome, and both implementations before making changes.

Preserve the existing experiments unchanged. Create v0.5a as a new sibling experiment.

---

## Conceptual proposition

The larger artistic proposition is now:

> **The Cosmos orders the Universe and thereby facilitates the creation of Worlds.**

For this experiment we are testing only the first relationship.

### Universe

Universe is physical contingency.

Its molten material is governed by behaviour such as:

- inertia;
- gravity;
- collision;
- accumulation;
- obstruction;
- storage;
- release;
- acceleration into the gravity well.

The `physical-layer` shared reservoir and phased sluice gates are important.

Many distributed streams feed a common store. A smaller number of controlled releases then send molten material towards the central casting point.

### Cosmos

Cosmos is generative order.

Its network expresses:

- relationship;
- connectivity;
- hierarchy;
- multiple scales of organisation;
- communication through persistent routes.

Its logical topology remains independent of the Casting Pit.

The pit may distort the **manifestation** of a Cosmic route because both systems inhabit the same spatial reality, but it must not determine its topology.

Information travels through the manifested route at essentially constant spatial speed, as established in the final `information-layer` work.

Gravity must not attract, redirect, accelerate or decelerate it.

---

## v0.5a experiment

Combine the successful v0.3 Physical system and the successful v0.4b Cosmic system on the same Casting Pit membrane.

Do not initially attempt to make the result beautiful by substantially changing either system.

We first need to discover what happens when they coexist.

The scene should therefore contain:

- the existing dark deformable membrane;
- the existing distributed molten inputs;
- the shared rim reservoir;
- the existing phased sluice gates;
- molten releases travelling towards the well;
- the v0.4b hierarchical Cosmic network;
- the improved comet-like communication packets;
- the existing gravity well.

The membrane is their shared physical setting.

The Cosmos should appear to belong to the same reality as the molten material rather than looking like an unrelated transparent graphic layer placed over it.

At the same time, Cosmos and Universe must remain perceptually distinct.

---

## Behavioural contrast

The strongest distinction should come from **behaviour**, not merely colour.

Molten material should remain:

- comparatively slow;
- viscous;
- accumulative;
- gravitational;
- capable of congestion and release;
- visibly affected by the depression.

Cosmic communication should remain:

- rapid;
- discrete;
- route-bound;
- essentially constant-speed;
- indifferent to gravitational attraction;
- capable of descending and climbing the manifested deformation without behaving as falling matter.

A particularly useful visual event would be a Cosmic communication packet traversing a conduit down one side of the gravity well and onwards while molten material nearby accelerates physically into the depression.

The viewer should be able to perceive:

> **One is falling. The other is going somewhere.**

## Important restraint

Do **not** yet implement:

- the v0.3b throat/opening;
- the Tree;
- Category Tree unification;
- semantic ERP data;
- new post-processing architecture;
- major redesign of the molten simulation;
- major redesign of the Cosmic topology.

Do not polish either subsystem in isolation during this experiment.

If combining them exposes a genuine incompatibility, document it rather than disguising it with glow, fog, particles or decorative effects.

The purpose is to test the relationship.

---

## Controls

Retain enough existing controls to inspect both systems properly.

In addition, provide a simple way to inspect:

1. **Universe only**
2. **Cosmos only**
3. **Universe + Cosmos**

This is important diagnostically.

Where practical, preserve the established useful defaults rather than retuning everything before we have seen the combined result.

The membrane diagnostic wireframe should remain available but off by default.

---

## Initial implementation approach

Treat the two existing experiments as source material rather than trying to refactor them into a grand reusable architecture before combining them.

Reuse working mechanisms where sensible.

Avoid premature abstraction.

The first objective is simply to produce a faithful combined experiment that we can inspect.

If technical conflicts between the two implementations require structural changes, make the minimum changes necessary and record them.

---

## What to inspect

Run the combined experiment and inspect it from both Elevated and Monumental viewpoints.

Pay particular attention to:

- whether Cosmos and Universe clearly occupy the same world;
- whether either layer visually destroys the other;
- whether molten accumulation and lavafalls remain readable;
- whether the hierarchy of the Cosmic network remains readable;
- whether the comet-like communications remain visible against molten material;
- whether the different motion laws are apparent;
- whether Cosmic routes crossing the gravity well remain convincing;
- whether dense regions become intolerable visual noise when combined;
- whether sparse regions provide enough visual rest;
- whether the central depression remains legible;
- whether the result feels monumental rather than diagrammatic;
- whether the combined scene suggests a larger world beyond the visible frame.

Do not assume visual collision is necessarily failure.

Some interference may make the shared reality more convincing.

- what had to change technically;
- what was deliberately preserved;
- visual observations;
- behavioural observations;
- performance observations;
- conflicts between the two systems;
- unexpected successes;
- unexpected failures;
- useful parameter states for human inspection.

Also create the v0.5a outcome document recording the implemented model and enough technical detail for us to understand how the combined code works and how it derives from v0.3a and v0.4b.

Do not mark the artistic proposition successful merely because the implementation runs.

The human review is important here.

---

## Acceptance criteria

v0.5a is technically successful if:

1. the physical system and information system coexist on the same Casting Pit membrane;
2. neither system has been conceptually rewritten merely to accommodate the other;
3. molten material retains physical/gravitational behaviour;
4. Cosmic communication retains route-bound constant manifested-speed behaviour;
5. Cosmic topology remains independent of the gravity well;
6. Universe-only, Cosmos-only and combined states can be inspected;
7. the experiment remains interactive and practically usable;
8. existing earlier experiments remain unchanged;
9. review and outcome documentation explain what was actually built and observed.

The artistic judgement is deliberately separate.

After implementation and inspection, stop for human review.

The question we will answer is:

> **Does Cosmic order imposed upon the Universe look magnificent, hideous, or interesting enough that we need to keep going?**

Do not begin the Tree or v0.3b throat.

Do not use post-processing, glow, bloom, fog or other decorative effects to disguise a structural or compositional failure in the combined scene.
