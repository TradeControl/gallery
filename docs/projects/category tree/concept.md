# Category Tree

## Origin and lineage

The artwork originates in the [Trade Control Category Tree](https://tradecontrol.github.io/cash/cash-configuration), a recursive classification system that turns heterogeneous operational activity into progressively larger financial and operational meanings.

Category Tree did not begin with shader code. An earlier AI examined that implementation, formed the word-picture **a windswept, recursive, engineered organism—not a tree, but tree-like**, and rendered an iterated static visual impression. That image was already one of Gallery's first four entries. It supplied the initial artistic proposition—not a technical specification—from which this concept, the separate component studies and the completed procedural composition were subsequently developed.

The lineage therefore retains Gallery's established four stages: **Analysis**, **Interpretation**, **Rendering** and **Iteration**. The later shader work extends the fourth stage into a new medium; it does not erase or rewrite the static work that preceded it.

## Artwork concept

The Category Tree is a monumental industrial, organic and computational artwork about dispersed activity becoming structured knowledge.

Across a dark constructed landscape, many separate luminous molten flows are drawn towards a central casting throat. The ground yields beneath their convergence: an immense surface pulled into a deep gravity-like depression, as though the accumulated weight of trade, work and material has deformed the world itself. The flows follow that deformation, gather energy and pour into the pit. From that same centre rises an engineered recursive structure—part tree, part foundry, part instrument—whose branches receive, classify and combine those flows into progressively larger meanings.

The work should feel discovered rather than diagrammed. It belongs to the visual world of furnaces, mineral surfaces, nervous systems, circuitry and ancient machinery. Its scale is architectural. Its motion is deliberate and consequential. Organic irregularity prevents it from becoming a sterile data graphic; computational order prevents it from becoming merely botanical.

The governing transformation is:

> Distributed operational flows converge, are cast into relationships, and emerge as recursive knowledge.

This is not a literal illustration of an accounting structure. It is an artistic embodiment of the Category Tree's essential act: heterogeneous terminal events are classified and recursively composed until they can be understood as operational and financial meaning.

## Four component studies, one composition

The completed artwork unifies four independently developed component studies. Each has its own conceptual and technical boundary, and each was required to become convincing before integration.

### [The Casting Pit](./casting-pit/concept.md)

The landscape is a dark, deformable membrane pulled towards a central depression. The pit is not a decorative crater. It is a field of consequence: distant material remains calm and planar; nearer material stretches, steepens and submits to the throat.

Separate luminous molten flows form the physical Universe. They travel across this surface, visibly follow its descent, converge and leave it at the centre. Beneath and around them, the ordered Cosmos carries information through a fine golden topology whose logic remains independent of the pit even while its spatial manifestation follows the shared terrain.

A grid, wireframe or equivalent inspection overlay may be used while developing the surface, but it is a diagnostic instrument rather than part of the finished image. Surface deformation, diagnostic markings, molten transport and information topology remain technically distinct systems even though they inhabit one Casting Pit.

### [The Tree](./tree/concept.md)

The Tree is a genuine recursive structure made from finite relationships: terminals, junctions, parent-child connections and higher confluences. Its topology must produce its form. Repetition and procedural texture may enrich that form, but must not substitute for actual branching.

The structure may resemble an engineered arbor, refinery, nervous system or computational foundry. It should be capable of showing many small inputs resolving through intermediate structures into larger totals. Branches are conduits with purpose; nodes are events or transformations, not incidental points of glow.

### [The Substrate](./substrate/concept.md)

The substrate is the energetic lower world beneath the finite Casting Pit terrain: a textureless dark-matter pressure sea rather than ordinary water, empty space or a second landscape. Its broad swells, crossing currents and localized energy give buried roots, the Cosmos and the gravity well a credible medium beyond the constructed surface.

It owns colour and motion beneath the terrain while the Casting Pit membrane owns visible ground and ordinary depth. Its warm and cold grazing light is reconstructed inside its own material, so the sea can disclose its form without relighting the Tree, the membrane or the information network.

### [The Superstrate](./superstrate/concept.md)

The superstrate is metaphysical emergence made visible, not sky, nebula or astronomical wallpaper. Four localized influences arise from the substrate, physical Universe, ordered information and living Tree. Each acts around its own source with its own reach, morphology and motion rather than washing the whole upper field uniformly.

Their pairwise overlaps generate **Confluence**: a fifth visible field but not a fifth source. It represents relation becoming perceptible—an emergent whole that cannot appear when only one influence is present.

### Their unification

The Tree as a whole is the transducer between the physical Universe and ordered Cosmos. Information descends through its recursive canopy and monumental trunk, transforms and continues into Cosmic circuitry. Molten material follows the opposite causal path: it is drawn from the physical Universe through the Casting Pit gravity well and rises around the trunk circumference to sustain the Tree. These processes meet compositionally but remain distinct transports with stable visual meanings.

The substrate supplies the energetic lower medium beneath that exchange. The superstrate manifests the four systems' localized fields of influence and the Confluence produced where they overlap. Its directions follow real anchors in the composition and change with the observer's view, although it deliberately exerts no numerical force back upon the lower simulations. The connection is metaphysical and compositional rather than a claim to physical causation.

The gravity well and buried root system form the hinge between landscape and structure. Their relationship should remain partly unseen and therefore larger than its finite representation. The viewer need not inspect internal machinery or read individual business terms to sense classification, passage and eventual transformation. Future connections to real Trade Control data may make the behaviour more specific, but the artwork must first succeed as an autonomous visual experience.

## Behaviour and meaning

Motion should explain the system rather than merely animate it. Pulses may enter through terminals, travel through finite conduits, combine at confluences, divide by type or polarity, dim when a subtree is dormant, or change course when a relationship changes. Contrasts such as warm and cool light should acquire stable meaning within a study rather than alternate decoratively.

The complete work may eventually explore Category Tree ideas including classification, recursive aggregation, polarity, selected reporting roots, disconnected structures and derived expressions. These are artistic possibilities, not a commitment to reproduce the whole application or expose every domain concept at once.

## Visual character

The desired image balances:

- Monumental scale with inspectable structure.
- Industrial weight with organic responsiveness.
- Computational precision with material imperfection.
- Darkness and restraint with concentrated emissive energy.
- A readable overall transformation with room for ambiguity and awe.

The camera should support both encounter and understanding: low or human-scale views can establish monumentality, while controlled movement and closer inspection can reveal deformation, topology and flow. Live controls first helped discover the useful artistic range and now form part of the completed interactive work. They allow the viewer to alter emphasis, energy, movement and scene without exposing every implementation constant or dissolving the composition into a technical dashboard.

## Provenance and discipline

The archived shaders and their assessments record an earlier attempt and the lessons it produced. They are evidence, not specifications. They preserve valuable instincts—mythic-industrial atmosphere, living computation, molten and circuit-like energy—while showing why the systems must now be separated and rebuilt deliberately.

In particular, the final work must not ask one distorted coordinate field to represent surface, diagnostic markings and flow, and must not ask geometric folding to impersonate recursive topology. Geometry, material coordinates and transport must agree where they are related, but should not be conflated into one coordinate distortion.

Each major system first became convincing on its own. The development sequence began with the four-stage static interpretation, separated the Casting Pit and Tree into independent construction problems, developed the substrate and superstrate away from the complex composition, and only then integrated the accepted systems into Category Tree. The false starts, disconnected studies and later unification are all part of the work's provenance.
