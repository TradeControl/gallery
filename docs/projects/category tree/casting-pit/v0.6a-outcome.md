# Casting Pit v0.6a — Implementation outcome

## Result

The approved historical-erosion throat has been implemented as a bounded physical-only experiment at:

    src/casting-pit/well-throat/

The signed-off physical and information references remain separate, and the evolving combined composition has been renamed to:

    src/casting-pit/universe-cosmos/

No v0.6a milestone copy of the combined composition was created.

## Implemented geometry

The new throat membrane retains the broad Gaussian gravity well and adds two deterministic influences within a compact central transition zone:

- low-frequency background erosion displaces and distorts the mouth, recesses the inner terrain and introduces broad shelf relief;
- flow-driven erosion selects up to three dominant historical approaches from the deterministic sluice-gate layout and cuts narrow recessed channels, with shallow eroded shoulders, from the surrounding terrain toward the mouth. Their curved bearings are produced by the gates' position and outlet bias.

The surface mesh has a real masked opening rather than a dark receiver disc. A separate uncapped wall mesh continues below that opening, tapers according to the throat-constriction control and terminates in darkness after the configured short depth. It does not attempt to model the deeper shaft.

## Transport and rendering

The existing causal transport sequence remains intact. Peripheral streams still enter the shared reservoir and only selected gates release stored parcels. No throat operation creates material or bypasses a gate.

After release, parcels are tested for crossing the irregular opening boundary and are captured there. The molten reconstruction uses a texture mask sampled from that same boundary, so reconstructed material does not bridge or cap the mouth. No falling-fluid or wall-bound lava behaviour has been introduced.

The shared sluice-gate layer gained only optional throat seams:

- a gate-layout callback for historical approach derivation;
- opening-crossing capture when supplied by a membrane;
- surface-mask clipping when supplied by a membrane;
- optional throat wall and diagnostic guide geometry.

Those paths are inactive for the signed-off Gaussian membrane. Its original circular receiver and molten shader path remain the default.

## Controls and diagnostics

The physical-only page provides live controls for mouth scale, background erosion, flow imprint, throat constriction and throat depth, plus a deterministic history reseed. The existing membrane, gate, transport, molten-field, Elevated, Monumental and free-orbit controls remain available.

The surface diagnostic now includes the throat wall and the selected historical approach guides. The throat experiment uses a denser diagnostic mesh so those channels remain legible without altering the signed-off reference diagnostic. Gate-layout reseeding or gate-count changes regenerate the historical approaches without changing the transport architecture.

## Verification

All four active pages produced successful Vite production builds:

- `physical-layer`
- `information-layer`
- `universe-cosmos`
- `well-throat`

Browser inspection confirmed:

- the v0.6a page loads without console errors;
- molten material renders and is clipped at the irregular mouth;
- parcels continue to accumulate in the reservoir and are released by the existing phased gates;
- mouth-scale and history-reseed controls update live;
- changing the gate count rebuilds both the gate system and the derived historical approach set;
- the signed-off physical reference still renders its established result;
- the information reference and renamed combined composition load without console errors.

The builds retain Vite's existing advisory that the Three.js bundles exceed 500 kB. This is not new to the throat experiment and no code-splitting work was introduced.

## Known limitations

- The visible terrain-opening edge is resolved against the existing regular membrane grid. The throat wall overlaps that edge to avoid a cap, but at close range some polygonal character remains. A later mesh-topology pass could create a contour-fitted annular patch if the current character proves too mechanical.
- The historical channels are intentionally few and directional. They are causally derived from a deterministic gate layout, but they are not a replay of current parcel trajectories.
- Reseeding the active gate layout changes the historical approach evidence as well as the present gates. This is useful diagnostically, but a later artistic pass may choose to decouple the two seeds while retaining the same derivation method.
- The throat wall is deliberately short, dry and uncapped. There is no material motion below the mouth and no Cosmos manifestation around the throat.
- The experiment has not been merged into `universe-cosmos`; the information layer therefore remains untouched until this physical result has been judged independently.
