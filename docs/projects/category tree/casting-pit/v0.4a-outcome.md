# Casting Pit v0.4a outcome — Cosmic Circuitry

6 September 2026

## Principal question

Does an intricate golden computational underlay carrying fast communication provide a useful fine-scale order beneath the coarse molten system?

**Yes, provisionally.** The isolated experiment establishes a visually compatible second frequency and a credible distinction between persistent structure and travelling information. It is not yet sufficiently rich or spatially varied to be treated as finished circuitry.

## Experiment

The new independent study is located at:

    src/casting-pit/cosmic-circuitry/

It contains only the dark deformable membrane, gravity well, established lighting and camera behaviour, optional diagnostic wireframe, procedural circuitry and route-bound communication. It was written as a fresh sibling rather than derived from the lava simulation. No packets, molten reconstruction, streams, reservoir, gates, throat or Tree were introduced. All earlier experiments remain unchanged.

## Lattice construction

The circuitry is a deterministic graph in its own two-dimensional material coordinate system. It is not a noise texture and does not reuse membrane topology.

The generator creates irregularly sampled concentric bands extending beyond the visible membrane. It deliberately removes sections to form incomplete arcs and dead ends, then adds short radial and tangential connections between neighbouring bands. Occasional same-band forks and chords provide local alternatives. Shared angular registration keeps most inter-band connections local; an early version gave every band an unrelated rotation and produced an incoherent spider-web of long crossings, so that approach was rejected during visual testing.

The default seed at complexity `0.58` produces 623 nodes and 880 connections. A dense test at `0.92` produced 992 nodes and 1,629 connections. Reseed generates a different deterministic network while retaining the same construction rules.

Graph coherence is explicit: every rendered segment refers to two graph nodes, and each node stores adjacency used by communication routes. There are no decorative lines that signals cannot conceptually traverse.

## Communication

Up to 140 signal routes are generated as finite random walks through graph adjacency. Each route stores cumulative edge lengths. A signal advances by physical route distance rather than by edge index, so it moves consistently across connections of different lengths.

Signals vary modestly in speed and initial phase. A minority begin after deterministic delays, creating local starts and quiet periods rather than one synchronized global pulse. They are rendered as tiny white-gold additive points above the finer, dimmer gold graph.

Communication density and speed are independent. Tests ranged from 12 slow signals at speed `1` to 120 signals at speed `7`. The sparse setting was contemplative but made communication easy to miss; the dense fast setting remained legible and made the informational/material contrast clearest.

## Relationship to the membrane

Pattern coordinates and spatial projection are separate.

The **Surface binding** control interpolates only the rendered height:

- `0` leaves the logical network on an undeformed mathematical plane;
- `1` projects it onto the gravity-well membrane;
- the default `0.82` allows the network to belong to the surface while retaining a slight sense of independence.

The flat Monumental comparison is conceptually strong: the gold plane visibly bridges above the descending diagnostic wireframe, proving that the circuitry is not generated by gravity deformation. Artistically, however, a fully flat plane looks detached and intersects the scene too literally. Values around `0.72–0.95` are the most convincing current underlay treatment.

## Controls

- **Circuitry intensity:** `0.05–1.5`, default `0.20`;
- **Density / complexity:** `0.15–1`, default `0.58`;
- **Circuitry scale:** `0.65–1.6`, default `1`;
- **Surface binding:** `0–1`, default `0.82`;
- **Communication density:** `0–140`, default `70`;
- **Communication speed:** `0.25–10`, default `4`;
- **Communication brightness:** `0.2–3`, default `0.3` after live review.

The established membrane depth, width, key light, exposure, camera views and optional wireframe remain. Reset restarts signal phasing without rebuilding the graph; Reseed deterministically rebuilds graph and routes.

## Visual tests

The first default used intensity `0.65` and complexity `0.72`. It was immediately rejected: additive line overlap overwhelmed the membrane and read as a bright astronomical web. Lowering the line intensity to `0.20`, reducing default complexity to `0.58`, and angularly registering neighbouring bands recovered the dark surface and made communication points the brightest events.

At the corrected default, the Elevated view reads as incomplete golden infrastructure descending through the well. Fine arcs, forks, radial connections and gaps remain coherent at distance. The structure extends beyond all visible edges and therefore suggests a larger unseen system.

At complexity `0.92`, intensity about `0.15`, 120 signals and speed `7`, the network remained readable rather than collapsing into luminous noise. The additional signals created convincing distributed information traffic. High circuitry intensity is not useful at high complexity.

At complexity `0.20`, intensity `0.40`, 12 signals and speed `1`, the individual paths and dead ends were clear but the network lacked sufficient richness and signals were easy to overlook.

Both camera views are useful. Elevated best explains topology and gravity association. Monumental makes the distinction between the flat mathematical plane and the deformed membrane especially clear. The optional wireframe correctly remains diagnostic and visually competes with the circuitry when enabled, confirming that it should remain off for artistic judgement.

## Performance

The default network is a static line buffer plus one small dynamic position buffer for at most 140 points. There is no per-frame graph generation, physics, neighbour search or texture reconstruction. Browser testing remained fluid at the dense 992-node / 1,629-edge / 120-signal setting, and no console errors or warnings were reported.

The production build succeeds. Vite issues only the repository's existing advisory that the Three.js bundle exceeds 500 kB.

## Visual strengths

- It introduces a genuinely finer scale than the lava without imitating molten material.
- Dim gold on graphite is compatible with the established Casting Pit palette.
- Bright beads clearly belong to routes rather than globally illuminating the surface.
- The graph continues beyond the visible territory.
- Incomplete arcs, forks and dead ends imply a designed system whose full purpose is unavailable.
- Separation of graph coordinates, spatial projection and communication avoids the archived coordinate-field conflation.
- Surface binding provides a useful direct test of “underlying order” versus “embedded marking.”

## Weaknesses and unwanted readings

- The present vocabulary leans towards an incomplete astronomical instrument or astrolabe. It does not yet fully achieve the requested aerial-city/metropolitan density contrast.
- Concentric organisation remains more apparent than recursive or district-scale structure.
- The graph has uniform line weight; hierarchy is encoded topologically but not yet visually.
- Dense and sparse regions arise mostly from missing connections rather than strong local architectural differentiation.
- High line intensity produces a generic sci-fi web and should not be used to manufacture richness.
- Fully flat projection demonstrates independence but looks like a separate plane floating through the pit.
- Signals currently follow predetermined random walks and disappear/repeat; junction branching is implied by concurrent traffic rather than enacted as a live split event.

## Relationship to lava

Interaction with different lava configurations was intentionally not tested because the brief explicitly requires the circuitry to succeed independently and forbids unification in v0.4a. The current colour, scale and depth treatment make later occlusion by broad lava plausible, but that remains a hypothesis for a separate experiment. No claim is made that the combined composition has yet succeeded.

## Acceptance assessment

1. **Different fine scale:** passed.
2. **Computational/cosmic infrastructure:** partially passed; coherent and non-PCB, but currently more astrolabe than metropolis.
3. **Gold, graphite and prospective lava compatibility:** passed in isolation; combined test deferred.
4. **Travelling rather than global communication:** passed.
5. **Fast informational character:** passed, especially at speeds `4–7`.
6. **Lava occlusion and identity:** not tested by design.
7. **Richness without noise:** passed at restrained intensity; fails at the original bright setting.
8. **Larger unseen system:** passed.
9. **Existing lava unchanged:** passed; its directory and implementation were not modified.

## Recommendation

**A. Refine the cosmic circuitry further.**

The concept has genuine traction and supplies the missing fine-scale order, but one more isolated refinement should introduce stronger local districts, subsidiary networks and visual hierarchy before the layer is combined with lava or used to inform the central transition. The present experiment is a credible foundation, not yet the final cosmic infrastructure.

## Immediate post-review refinement — grooves and electrical discharge

Live review identified two unwanted readings in the first implementation: the uniform one-pixel graph resembled a spider's web, and communication beads read as bright objects travelling over it rather than electricity moving through it.

Each connection is now subdivided into a slightly irregular polyline. Beneath the fine gold trace, the renderer constructs a narrow dark-bronze triangular ribbon whose width varies deterministically along the edge. Together, the dark shoulders, uneven centre line and changing width make the network read as incised conductive grooves or ancient channels in the membrane rather than weightless thread. The groove geometry is still generated from the graph; it is not a texture or unrelated decorative displacement.

Point-sprite communication has been removed. Each event is now a six-segment white-gold discharge occupying a short length of its route. Brightness falls from the leading edge through the tail, trail length grows modestly with speed, and a restrained global flicker changes its electrical energy without flashing the lattice itself. The discharge follows cumulative route distance and can cross graph-edge boundaries continuously.

The corrected default retained the existing restrained network values. A denser Monumental stress test at complexity `0.85`, 105 discharges and speed `7` produced 955 nodes and 1,574 connections. The rough grooves remained individually readable and the electrical strokes did not collapse into a field of dots or a global dashed pattern. No browser errors or warnings were reported.

This refinement materially reduces the spider-web reading and improves the distinction between infrastructure and communication. It does not by itself solve the broader metropolitan question: concentric organisation is still dominant, so district-scale topology and hierarchy remain the appropriate subject of a later refinement.

## Reference-informed district refinement

The supplied static Category Tree image was treated only as visual evidence about the desired ground character, not as a composition to reproduce. Its useful lesson is that ordered structure can feel excavated from a material surface when continuity, hierarchy and visibility are uneven. The relevant qualities are a few substantial routes, broken families of subordinate marks, local concentrations, irregular spacing and large areas where the system is only inferred.

The generated network now has three explicit visual tiers. A small number of trunk grooves cross several radial bands and establish large-scale districts. Intermediate branches connect those trunks into the existing graph. Fine contour-like traces remain local and frequently incomplete. Each edge also receives a deterministic visibility value, so some regions are legible while others are almost absorbed into the graphite membrane. Groove width, trace brightness and continuity all follow this hierarchy; the previous uniformly exposed rings no longer dominate the composition.

This is still one coherent mathematical graph. The low-visibility edges have not been removed from its topology, and electrical discharges continue to traverse the complete route system. Consequently a moving discharge can briefly reveal a buried connection instead of merely decorating an already luminous line. The user-preferred communication brightness of `0.3` is now the default, making those events restrained electrical disclosures rather than bright travelling objects.

Browser review in both Monumental and Elevated views showed a materially different reading: dark, rough-edged infrastructure organised into partial districts rather than an evenly presented radial web. The major routes remain comprehensible at a distance, while finer marks recede into the surface and the centre retains its gravitational pull. No console warnings or errors were reported.

This refinement addresses the principal web-like failure without borrowing the reference image's tree silhouette, terminal squares, vertical composition or specific route layout. The next useful judgement remains artistic rather than technical: whether this balance of exposed trunks and buried local structure is sufficiently metropolitan before the circuitry is ever tested beside lava.

## Living-connectivity refinement

Further review exposed an over-correction in the district treatment. Although its topology had improved, the infrastructure had become passive: narrow dim grooves were merely illuminated by informational events. That contradicted the intended relationship. The ordered ground is cosmos and the incoming feed is universe, but both must possess comparable luminosity; the network itself requires subliminal fire.

Channel widths have therefore increased by roughly sixty per cent across all three hierarchy tiers. A new persistent ember core occupies the inner portion of every visible groove. It uses an orange-red heat palette, varies in width and temperature along each rough segment, and undergoes a restrained compound pulse. The existing dark groove remains around it as an incised shoulder, while the fine gold trace supplies a hotter centre seam.

Travelling electrical discharges remain independent white-gold events inside these channels. They modulate and momentarily intensify an already living system rather than acting as its only illumination. Circuitry intensity now defaults to `0.55`; communication brightness remains at the reviewed `0.3`, preserving the distinction between distributed subsurface heat and sharper information movement.

Browser inspection confirmed that the primary routes now read as luminous channels rather than hairline graph edges. Their varying heat gives the metropolitan network a persistent metabolism, while the incomplete district visibility and buried subsidiary routes remain intact. No browser errors or warnings were reported.
