# Casting Pit v0.1 outcome

5 September 2026

## Result

The v0.1 study answers its question positively: a continuous subdivided surface can read as a broad membrane drawn down by a central force from both a low monumental view and a clearer elevated inspection view.

The implementation uses one Gaussian radial height mapping for both the dense shaded membrane and the lower-density diagnostic mesh. The overlay therefore remains attached during live depth and width changes. Inspection showed no independent sliding, radial bands, oval artefacts, holes or visible normal discontinuities.

## Useful ranges

- Depression depth: approximately `4.5` to `6.5` gives a substantial but still membrane-like descent. Values up to `7.0` remain geometrically stable but begin to read as a severe shaft when paired with a narrow width.
- Depression width: approximately `3.2` to `4.5` keeps the centre authoritative while leaving a broad calm outer field. Wider values soften the sense of a concentrated force; narrower values produce a more abrupt well.
- Key light: approximately `3.0` to `5.0` reveals the changing normals without flattening the dark material.
- Exposure: approximately `0.9` to `1.15` preserves the restrained industrial surface while retaining slope detail.
- The default combination—depth `5.5`, width `3.8`, key light `4.0`, exposure `1.0`—is a useful balanced starting point.

## Camera findings

- The Monumental preset uses a low oblique view. It retains the horizon and presents the depression primarily through scale, silhouette and the lit opposing slope.
- The Elevated preset is the more reliable view for judging continuity, profile, normals and diagnostic attachment.
- Orbit, zoom and pan allow intermediate views without imposing automated camera motion.

## Limitations for later phases

- The mapping is deliberately a heightfield and cannot form an opening, vertical throat or undercut. Any central transition should remain a separate v0.3 decision informed by the lava study.
- No inward material contraction is implemented. The study proves vertical deformation and attached material coordinates without adding another variable.
- The diagnostic overlay is a triangular wire mesh. It is effective for exposing geometry and attachment, but it is not intended as finished surface decoration.
- The surface is intentionally uniform and restrained. It has no lava, channels, procedural material detail, atmosphere or post-processing.
- Very deep, narrow settings remain stable but cease to convey a broad flexible membrane convincingly. Lava work in v0.2 should begin from the balanced range rather than those extremes.
