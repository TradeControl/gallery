# Casting Pit v0.2b-2 outcome — Molten Reconstruction

6 September 2026

## Question tested

Can the proven Variable Packet Streams motion be reconstructed as continuous molten material without replacing or disguising its emergent dynamics?

The answer is provisionally yes. The reconstruction crosses from visible particles to a shared surface material, particularly at confluences and accumulations, while exposing a remaining material-design problem along exceptionally coherent trajectories.

## Implementation

The new study lives in:

    src/casting-pit/molten/

It is derived from `variable-packets/`. The existing ribbon, parcel and variable-packet studies remain unchanged.

### Reconstruction field

The molten layer is a CPU-maintained **224 × 224** scalar field in the membrane's material-space coordinates. Each cell stores:

- density, representing deposited material history;
- heat, deposited in proportion to packet temperature and later evaluated relative to density.

The field is uploaded as a floating-point RGBA `DataTexture`. Red contains density and green contains heat; the unused channels keep the representation simple and directly inspectable.

### Deposition

Every packet retains its previous position for the current fixed step. Deposition samples the complete segment from that position to its new position at spacing derived from the field texel size. This swept deposition prevents fast packets from leaving dotted or disconnected histories.

Each sample uses a small Gaussian-like brush with a two-texel radius. Contribution is proportional to packet mass, packet temperature, elapsed simulation time and the live deposition control. Depositing the existing moving packets repeatedly allows coherent populations to build broad streams while crowded packets create denser shared regions.

No new trajectory, steering, source choreography or capture force was introduced.

### Persistence, cooling and spreading

The field is reconstructed at 30 Hz, independently of rendering. Each reconstruction update:

1. applies exponential density decay using the selected persistence time;
2. cools heat somewhat faster than density;
3. performs a restrained four-neighbour diffusion step for local spreading;
4. uploads the updated density and heat texture.

This leaves visible history and joins neighbouring packet traces without permanently painting the membrane. It is deliberately not a fluid solver: spreading is local reconstruction of the packet-supported material, not a new transport system.

### Material and shader

A second copy of the membrane geometry is deformed by the same height function and carries the molten shader. The field is sampled by the membrane's stable UV/material coordinates, so molten material remains attached when the depression dimensions change.

Density gives the layer slight physical relief above the membrane. The fragment shader provides:

- very dark red cooled material at thin edges;
- dull red-orange bodies;
- restrained brighter orange only where density and heat are both high;
- procedural tonal breakup resembling a cooling crust;
- darker field-gradient edges which reinforce thickness;
- transparent discard below the reconstruction threshold.

The first shader pass was uniformly bright and smooth and read too much like orange tubing. Its correction was limited to material response: lower emission, dark cooled edges, local crust variation and restricted hot cores. No post-processing or bloom was added.

## Controls

The Variable Packet Streams controls remain, including **Number of streams** from 2–12, constant total emission, deterministic reseeding, gravity, drag and interaction.

Four focused molten controls were added:

- **Persistence**: 1–12 seconds, default 5;
- **Deposition**: 0.25–2.5, default 1;
- **Molten intensity**: 0.35–2, default 0.85;
- **Reconstruction threshold**: 0.02–0.4, default 0.10.

Changing stream count or seed resets both packets and the reconstruction field cleanly. Resetting replays the same deterministic arrangement.

## Diagnostic mode

Packets, source colours and initial-direction markers are hidden in the normal view. **Packet diagnostics** reveals them over the molten layer. Velocity vectors remain separately selectable and only appear while packet diagnostics are active. The membrane wireframe remains independent.

Visual comparison confirmed that packet positions and velocity histories lie within the reconstructed material. Broad molten regions correspond to packet accumulation rather than an unrelated shader animation.

## Visual observations

Tests covered 2, 6 and 12 streams; source seeds 1 and 2; default and low gravity; persistence values 2, 5 and 8; and both Elevated and Monumental cameras.

### Two streams

At the default total input of 48 packets per second, two sources reconstruct into a few substantial bodies. Seed 1 retained the variable-source result: the inward population made a broad continuous descent and captured strongly, while the tangential population accumulated and eventually escaped. The result reads as a small number of heavy incoming flows rather than two sparse dotted trains.

The coherent inward history is also where the present weakness is most apparent. A long isolated flow can retain nearly constant width and resemble an extruded ribbon. Crust variation and cooling help, but do not completely remove that reading.

### Six streams

At default settings, separate oblique histories remain legible on the outer field, then widen or join where packets dwell and meet. Seed 1 produced several captures plus two long-lived populations. Seed 2 produced a different but credible set of confluences and one outward-component failure.

The central convergence is materially clearer than in the packet view: overlapping trajectories create a shared red-orange body without preserving source colours. The receiver still removes the supporting particles cleanly, but the absence of a real throat becomes visible as material history converges at a plain dark centre.

### Twelve streams

With the total input still fixed at 48, twelve sources create thinner distributed arrivals and substantially more crossings. At seed 1 the sampled packet system produced 1,280 cross-source neighbour pairs after approximately 18 seconds. The molten rendering merged these histories into thicker branches and local patches rather than twelve permanently separate coloured cables.

The Monumental camera makes distributed entry and central submission convincing, although foreshortening can make some detached outer accumulations appear abruptly terminated. Seed 2 produced different confluences while preserving the overall progression from fragmented outer activity to shared material near the depression.

The **Number of streams** control remains meaningful: few streams produce fewer, broader bodies; many streams divide the same input into finer arrivals which cross and recombine. It does not merely alter brightness or total material volume.

## Pooling

At gravity `1.6`, persistence `8`, six streams and seed 2, the live parcel population reached 496 / 500. The reconstruction showed wide off-centre reservoirs at slow and intersecting regions, feeding narrower downstream material. Five populations continued capturing while the outward population escaped (12 losses in the observed run).

These accumulations are more convincing as molten pools than they were as coloured particle queues because they become shared, locally spread bodies with hotter replenished areas and darker cooling margins. Some visibly drain or change outline as packet traffic moves through them.

The qualification is important: at or near the 500-packet cap, a heavily congested population can reconstruct into a blunt slab. That reveals genuine dynamics congestion as well as useful accumulation. The reconstruction does not prove that every packet backlog is a desirable pool.

At persistence `2`, old history clears more quickly and the same seed produces narrower, more local bodies. Continuity remains adequate at the default deposition rate. Persistence `5` is the best tested compromise; `8` is useful for inspecting reservoirs but exaggerates stale history under congestion.

## Surface attachment and convergence

The molten geometry uses the same 180-segment membrane construction and height deformation as the base surface. Texture lookup is in material space rather than projected screen space, so flows stay attached to the slope and continue into the depression from either camera.

Outer motion remains free: oblique, tangential and outward histories are visible, and some escape. Near the steeper gradient, compatible packet histories bend and merge towards the receiver. The reconstruction therefore reveals capture rather than making every source radial from birth.

## Performance

The field contains 50,176 cells and updates at 30 Hz. Observed reconstruction cost was approximately **0.8–2.2 ms** per field update. Total fixed-step simulation plus deposition and occasional reconstruction was generally **2–7 ms** at high parcel counts on this machine. Interaction remained responsive at the 500-packet cap.

No browser errors or warnings were observed. The production build succeeds, with only Vite's existing advisory that the Three.js bundle is larger than 500 kB.

## Explicit assessment

**Does the reconstructed system now read as molten material rather than electronic transmission?**

Mostly yes. Shared colour, density-dependent width and relief, dark cooling edges, hot replenished cores, crust variation, persistence and merging create an embodied surface material. Pools and confluences cross the conceptual boundary most convincingly. Long isolated coherent trajectories can still read as smooth ribbons, so the material model is promising rather than finished.

**Did molten reconstruction preserve the emergent behaviour demonstrated by the Variable Packet Streams experiment?**

Yes. Source generation, emission normalisation and all motion forces are unchanged. The molten field is deposited only from packet movement, and diagnostic overlay confirms the correspondence. Different stream counts, seeds and gravity settings retain their previous capture, escape, crossing and dwell-time differences.

**Do packet accumulations become useful material pools, or do they reveal a dynamics/congestion problem?**

Both, depending on severity. Moderate accumulation becomes useful shared pools which thicken, cool and receive or release material. Sustained accumulation near the fixed particle cap produces overly blunt slabs and exposes real congestion. This does not yet justify revisiting the dynamics, but future material refinement should avoid disguising the distinction.

## Visual weaknesses

- Long stable packet lanes can reconstruct with overly consistent width and retain a ribbon-like silhouette.
- Diffusion spreads material locally but cannot reproduce fluid surface motion inside a static pool.
- Crust breakup is spatial rather than advected, appropriate for this bounded study but not a final material model.
- Dense congestion can become a slab rather than a naturally spreading pool.
- The temporary receiver leaves an abrupt dark termination; this belongs to the later throat study.
- The membrane material and lighting remain intentionally plain, limiting the sense of reflected molten light.

## Recommendation

**A. Continue refining molten reconstruction.**

The underlying behaviour remains credible and the material model is moving in the right direction. The next bounded work should concentrate on width variation, internal pool motion and less ribbon-like silhouettes while continuing to derive every visible history from the existing packet field. The central throat should wait until the molten body is sufficiently settled that its transition requirements are clear.
