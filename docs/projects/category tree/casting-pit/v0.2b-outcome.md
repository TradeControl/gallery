# Casting Pit v0.2b reconnaissance outcome

5 September 2026

## Recommendation

**B. Decompose v0.2b further.**

Emergent molten flow appears feasible in real time with the existing native JavaScript, Three.js and Vite stack. The smallest credible direction is a hybrid of surface-constrained moving parcels and a temporary material-space density field. It is still two distinct questions: whether a small interaction model produces consequential trajectories, and whether those trajectories can be reconstructed as coherent molten material. Solving both at once would make it difficult to distinguish a dynamics failure from a rendering failure.

The recommended sequence is:

1. **v0.2b-1 — Parcel dynamics.** Render diagnostic parcels plainly and prove gravity, momentum, crossing, interaction, convergence and stable controls. This is an explicitly diagnostic study, not the finished appearance.
2. **v0.2b-2 — Molten reconstruction.** Deposit successful parcel motion into a short-lived density and heat field, then render that field as connected surface-bound material.

Do not begin with GPU simulation, full fluid dynamics, permanent rough terrain or engineered channels.

## 1. Why v0.2 reads as cables

In v0.2, each cubic Bézier curve already contains the complete answer to where a flow has been and where it will go. The ribbon is continuously present along that answer. Animation changes brightness along the ribbon but does not move material or alter the route.

This makes trajectory primary and material secondary. Constantly smooth widths, clean edges, periodic luminous pulses and immutable paths reinforce the visual language of cables carrying signals. Two ribbons may overlap visually, but neither can change the other's position, velocity, width or future. The system therefore depicts transmission rather than material consequence.

This is not a technical defect. It is a successful surface-bound signal system whose visual language may suit the later engineered Tree better than pre-casting molten material.

## 2. Real-time feasibility

A deliberately limited emergent model is feasible on the CPU. A target of roughly `300–800` active parcels, a fixed simulation step around `1/60` second, and a uniform spatial hash for neighbour lookup should be modest for a contemporary desktop browser. Interaction cost should remain approximately proportional to parcel count and nearby neighbours rather than comparing every parcel with every other parcel.

A `192×192` or `256×256` material-space density texture is also practical. A `256×256` single-channel field contains only 65,536 cells; even an RGBA byte texture is about 256 KiB per upload. The exact limit must be measured rather than assumed, especially when CPU splatting, neighbour interaction and Three.js rendering occur in the same frame.

The proposal is not computational fluid dynamics. It is a controlled particle-and-field metaphor designed to produce the few behaviours the artwork requires.

## 3. Smallest proposed computational model

Use continuously emitted parcels moving in the membrane's two-dimensional material coordinates `(x,z)`.

At each fixed simulation step:

1. Evaluate the membrane height gradient at each parcel position.
2. Accelerate the parcel downhill along the surface.
3. Preserve its previous velocity so momentum can carry it across the otherwise radial field.
4. Apply moderate drag appropriate to viscous material.
5. Query nearby parcels through a uniform spatial hash.
6. Apply a small set of local interactions: short-range separation/pressure to prevent singular collapse, velocity alignment/viscosity to make nearby material entrain, and mild cohesion within a finite radius.
7. Integrate position with a stable fixed time step.
8. Remove or recycle parcels that reach the central receiver, cool excessively, age out or leave the membrane.

Sources should emit at different positions with unequal but controlled initial velocity, volume and cadence. Their initial conditions begin the motion; no path is authored.

Semi-implicit Euler integration is sufficient for the first diagnostic study. A fixed accumulator should decouple simulation behavior from display frame rate, with a cap on catch-up steps after a stalled tab.

## 4. State carried by each moving unit

The minimum useful parcel state is:

- Material-space position `(x,z)`.
- Material-space velocity `(vx,vz)`.
- Effective volume or mass, used for deposition and momentum weighting.
- Temperature or heat, used later for cooling and emitted intensity.
- Age or remaining lifetime.
- Source identity, retained for diagnostics and controlled variation rather than path selection.

World-space height is derived from `membraneHeight(x,z)` and should not be stored independently. This prevents parcels from drifting away from a membrane whose live depth or width changes.

Previous position may be temporarily useful for swept collision/deposition, but long history should not be required by the dynamics model.

## 5. Membrane influence

For the current Gaussian heightfield `h(x,z)`, calculate its gradient analytically or by a small centred difference. The downhill direction is `-∇h`.

A useful surface acceleration is proportional to:

    -gravity * gradient / (1 + dot(gradient, gradient))

The denominator keeps acceleration well behaved on steep slopes and approximates projecting gravity onto the surface parameterisation. The exact scale is an artistic control, not a claim of physical units.

The smooth membrane is sufficient for the first dynamics proof if sources provide different initial velocities and parcels preserve momentum. Near the outer field the gradient is weak, so initial motion matters; descending into the well, the growing radial acceleration should progressively dominate and produce convergence.

## 6. Crossing, merging and interaction

On a two-dimensional surface, simultaneous streams cannot physically pass through one another without interaction. When parcels from different sources enter the same neighbourhood:

- viscosity/alignment moves their velocities toward a volume-weighted local mean;
- cohesion keeps a stream from immediately atomising;
- short-range pressure prevents all parcels collapsing into one point;
- preserved momentum allows an oblique stream to deflect another before the central slope dominates both.

This permits joining, deflection and temporary accumulation without explicit hard-coded merge events. In the rendered density field, overlapping parcel populations naturally become one wider, hotter region. Source identities can remain available diagnostically even when their visible material merges.

Do not initially delete two nearby parcels and replace them with one. Hard merging complicates conservation, source cadence and parcel-count stability before it is known to be visually necessary.

## 7. Rendering continuous molten material

Diagnostic points are acceptable for v0.2b-1 only. For v0.2b-2, deposit each parcel's volume and temperature into a short-lived density/heat field in the membrane's material coordinates.

The simplest reconstruction is:

- A `192×192` or `256×256` `DataTexture` covering the existing `24×24` membrane.
- Compact smooth kernels splatted around parcel positions.
- Controlled decay between steps, leaving a short viscous wake rather than permanent trails.
- Optional deposition along the swept segment from previous to current position to prevent gaps at higher velocity.
- A second membrane-shaped render mesh, offset only enough to avoid depth fighting, whose shader samples density and heat.
- A soft density threshold for connected bodies, hotter emissive cores, darker cooling edges and modest width variation.

Because this is a separate transport texture sampled by a separate overlay material, the simulation remains distinct from membrane deformation and the diagnostic grid. The overlay geometry should use the same height mapping as v0.1, preserving attachment.

If CPU field generation proves expensive, parcel splats can later be rendered into a low-resolution WebGL render target. That is an optimisation path, not the starting architecture.

## 8. Is small-scale surface structure required?

Not initially.

The smooth membrane's radial symmetry would make parcels released from rest follow orderly radial lines. Differing source positions alone do not solve this. Differing initial tangential velocities, momentum, uneven volume/cadence and neighbour interaction are the minimum justified sources of variation.

If these still settle into sterile paths, add a weak, low-frequency, spatially coherent lateral force field in material coordinates. It should be static or very slowly evolving so a parcel experiences terrain-like influence rather than frame-to-frame jitter. Its scale should be several channel widths but much smaller than the gravity well, and its force should diminish on the steep inner slope so monumental convergence remains dominant.

Only if that force clearly improves behavior should it be interpreted as shallow permanent surface structure. Modifying visible geometry or allowing flow-created deformation would add a separate construction problem and is not justified for the first study. Unstructured noise is not a substitute for interaction.

## 9. Performance and complexity

The dynamics proof is moderate rather than trivial:

- Fixed-step parcel integration is small and understandable.
- A spatial hash is the first necessary data structure; naïve all-pairs interaction is unacceptable beyond a small diagnostic count.
- Neighbour parameters are sensitive and can become an implementation dashboard. Expose only gravity, momentum/drag, interaction strength and source rate initially.
- The density reconstruction adds a second performance budget: clearing/decaying the field, splatting kernels and uploading texture data.

Reasonable initial budgets are `300–500` parcels, a `192×192` field, a neighbour radius around two to four intended channel widths, and no more than two fixed simulation steps per rendered frame under normal operation. Instrument frame time and active parcel count during development, but do not make those diagnostics part of the artwork.

## 10. Existing v0.2 work to retain

Retain unchanged:

- The v0.1 membrane geometry and `membraneHeight(x,z)` source of truth.
- Normal recalculation, lighting, tone mapping, resize and DPR handling.
- Monumental and elevated views plus `OrbitControls`.
- The switchable diagnostic overlay.
- The live-control panel pattern.
- The provisional central receiver as a temporary termination radius.
- The broad material and colour relationship: dark restrained membrane with concentrated warm energy.

Preserve the Bézier-ribbon implementation as historical working code rather than deleting it. The cleanest implementation session would copy the present v0.2 experiment into a sibling experimental entry or retain it behind a deliberately temporary comparison switch. A new sibling is preferable because it avoids turning the control panel into version management.

The present ribbon shader's colour treatment may inform the later density renderer, but its periodic travelling-wave logic should not drive parcel motion.

## 11. Expected failure modes

- Parcels collapse into dense clumps because cohesion exceeds pressure.
- Parcels disperse into glowing spray because pressure, stochastic force or initial velocity is too strong.
- Interaction produces numerical jitter or explosive velocities at high local density.
- Every source still becomes radial because downhill acceleration overwhelms momentum too early.
- Excess momentum produces an accretion-disc spiral rather than convergence.
- Strong drag removes consequence and makes trajectories look procedurally steered.
- Density decay is too fast, exposing dots and broken dashes; too slow, producing static painted trails.
- The density threshold causes width popping as streams meet.
- Low field resolution produces blockiness; high resolution makes CPU splatting and upload too expensive.
- Live changes to the membrane create abrupt parcel acceleration or visual discontinuity. Positions remain attached, but velocities may need clamping during large edits.
- The render becomes spectacular before the interactions are credible, hiding a dynamics failure beneath emissive styling.

These are reasons to keep v0.2b-1 visually diagnostic.

## 12. One experiment or smaller studies?

Use two smaller studies.

### v0.2b-1: dynamics and consequence

Prove that multiple sources on the smooth membrane can produce non-prescribed trajectories, inward dominance, interaction, deflection and accumulation. Render source-coloured parcels and optional velocity vectors. Acceptance should concern motion and consequence, not lava appearance.

### v0.2b-2: density and molten continuity

Freeze the successful dynamics parameter range, then prove deposition, temporal persistence, connected stream reconstruction, temperature appearance and surface attachment. Acceptance should concern continuity, material reading and performance.

Only after both succeed should they replace the v0.2 ribbons in the main Casting Pit experiment.

## 13. Engineered foundry channels

Engineered channels should remain a fallback, not the preferred next step.

They offer strong physical legitimacy, direct artistic control, stable performance and an industrial visual language. They also return trajectory authority to the designer and risk repeating the current cable problem in a more literal form. A hybrid of prepared channels, overtopping and local free-flow interaction could ultimately be powerful, especially if completely free flow is visually incoherent on the smooth membrane.

The proposed parcel study is small enough to test before accepting that compromise. If v0.2b-1 cannot produce stable joining and deflection without excessive tuning, the evidence would then support moving to a hybrid foundry model rather than escalating toward full fluid simulation.
