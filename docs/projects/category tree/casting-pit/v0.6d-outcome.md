# Casting Pit v0.6d — mouth-crossing continuity outcome

**Date:** 27 September 2026  
**Status:** rejected after visual review; do not carry forward

## Outcome

v0.6d implemented the four-stage experiment proposed in `v0.6d review.md`, but the experiment is rejected after visual review.

The principal structural result is successful: the grey rock-like form is no longer present when the throat is inactive. The upper throat now changes continuously from the membrane material at the attachment to a much darker interior response below it. The shaft remains open, uncapped and centred on the canonical well frame.

The active result is deliberately more visible than v0.6c. Real gate-released parcels produce a short molten crossing record and a warm response in a depth-banded part of the throat. The effect stops completely when crossings stop or when its two diagnostic switches are disabled.

The visual review nevertheless found that the active response reads as a **cartoon fire in the singularity**. It replaces one unwanted central object with another and competes directly with the tree that is intended to occupy the well. Its causality and implementation correctness do not rescue the artistic result.

The elevated camera also compresses genuine downward motion against the projected rim. A long, correctly depth-tested fall disappears behind the near throat wall; drawing the same fall without depth testing places it falsely over the foreground. The bounded compromise therefore cannot produce the required depth cue without acquiring an intrusive object-like character.

The experiment should be retained only as diagnostic history. Its molten traces, throat glow and depth-aware throat treatment must not be adopted by the continuing composition.

## Final artistic decision

v0.6d is abandoned.

The decisive failure is not technical subtlety or insufficient brightness. The proposed cure introduces the wrong subject at the most important point in the composition. A fiery singularity would be visually redundant once the tree is planted and would compete with, rather than support, the Category Tree.

Work therefore returns to the pre-throat Universe/Cosmos composition at:

`src/category-tree/casting-pit/universe-cosmos/index.html`

That entry uses the shared Gaussian membrane and contains neither the eroded-throat geometry nor the v0.6d mouth-crossing effects. It is the appropriate base for the next compositional step.

## Stage 1 — depth-aware throat

`createThroatGeometry()` now emits two additional vertex attributes:

- `throatDepth`, normalised through the complete open shaft;
- `color`, which reduces the ordinary terrain response rapidly below the attachment.

The throat material uses those vertex colours. At the top ring it still matches the membrane; through the controlled throat it darkens smoothly to six per cent of that response. The deep continuation remains dark.

This treatment removes the over-lit upper-wall fragment that formerly read as a buried grey rock. It does not add or remove any throat geometry.

## Stage 2 — exact crossing events

The former boolean `crossesOpening()` operation is now backed by `findOpeningCrossing()`.

The function:

1. samples the parcel's final swept segment at the established 0.04-unit interval;
2. identifies the first outside-to-inside interval;
3. bisects that interval eight times;
4. returns the crossing position and segment ratio.

The existing capture decision is unchanged: every unheld parcel still uses the same opening predicate and is removed under the same conditions. A visual throat event is recorded only when that captured parcel has previously passed through `releaseParcel()` and therefore came from a selected gate.

Each retained visual record inherits:

- the actual crossing position;
- horizontal crossing velocity;
- source colour;
- temperature;
- mass.

It has no force, collision, deposition or feedback into the physical simulation. At most 60 records, represented by four instanced samples each, can exist at once.

## Stage 3 — event-derived interior response

Active records contribute a bounded energy value derived from mass, temperature and remaining lifetime.

That value drives two restrained responses:

- a warm point light positioned below the mouth;
- a separate additive throat surface whose shader is visible only within a depth band below the terrain attachment.

The explicit rim is excluded from the glow band, preventing the response from becoming a permanent luminous collar. The energy rises quickly during crossings and decays to exactly zero afterwards. No minimum glow is maintained.

The first implementation attempt applied emission to the complete throat material. Browser comparison showed that this merely recoloured the former projected rock orange. That version was rejected. The final shader uses the new depth coordinate and leaves the base throat material non-emissive.

## Stage 4 — adversarial visual findings

The implementation was exercised in the running Vite page with the default elevated view and with live gate traffic.

### Inactive state

With no active crossing response, the former grey rock is absent. The opening reads as a clean dark shaft. There is no cap, terminal ring, collar or replacement object.

### Active state

At the default settings, the gate and capture diagnostics confirmed sustained causal traffic. One observed sample after approximately 28 seconds reported:

- 849 gate releases;
- 735 throat crossings;
- a closest released approach inside the opening by approximately 0.06 world units during the temporary diagnostic pass.

A later one-second observation recorded 30 additional crossing events. These figures were used to prove that the response was driven by live captures rather than a timer or decorative source. Only the cumulative gate-release and throat-crossing counters remain in the interface.

### Visibility experiments

Several rendering variants were compared and rejected during implementation:

- a whole-throat emissive response recreated the central object in orange;
- a long depth-tested droplet train was physically occluded by the near wall;
- the same long train without depth testing visibly overpainted foreground terrain;
- a camera-centred trace concentrated into another bright knot.

The retained compromise is shorter and more local. Explicit molten samples mark the crossing moment, while the depth-banded wall response conveys that the cavity has received energy. Camera-facing selection prevents every crossing around the circumference from becoming a screen-space cluster.

## Controls and diagnostics

The well-throat page now exposes two independent switches:

- **Captured molten traces**;
- **Event-driven illumination**.

The statistics panel also reports cumulative:

- gate releases;
- throat crossings.

These counters reset with the existing process reset and provide a direct causality check during review.

## Preserved behaviour

No changes were made to:

- source generation;
- reservoir capture and storage;
- gate selection, phase or release rate;
- parcel forces, drag or interaction;
- molten deposition and reconstruction before the mouth;
- the opening predicate;
- historical erosion geometry;
- throat radius, constriction or continuation geometry;
- camera controls;
- Cosmos, the information layer or Category Tree work.

The only new parcel state is a boolean recording whether the parcel has passed through an actual gate. It controls visual continuation only; it does not change capture eligibility.

## Verification

- JavaScript syntax checks pass for the modified modules.
- A Vite production build of the now-archived well-throat experiment succeeded before archival.
- The page runs without browser warnings or errors.
- Reset clears active traces, illumination and the two cumulative counters.
- Disabling both continuity switches leaves the depth-treated open shaft without a replacement central object.
- The existing molten surface remains masked by the established opening.

## Recommendation

Do not refine or integrate v0.6d.

Return to `src/category-tree/casting-pit/universe-cosmos/index.html` as the active composition. It is the nearest established render before the throat experiments and remains suitable for planting and developing the tree.

Keep the v0.6d implementation and this outcome only as evidence of a rejected direction. Do not increase brightness, droplet count or throat illumination, and do not transplant these mechanisms into Universe/Cosmos.
