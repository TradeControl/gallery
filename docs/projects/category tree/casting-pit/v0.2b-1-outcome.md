# Casting Pit v0.2b-1 outcome

5 September 2026

## Recommendation

**A. Proceed to v0.2b-2 molten reconstruction.**

The diagnostic study demonstrates that surface-constrained parcels can develop non-prescribed, momentum-dependent trajectories, interact with neighbouring populations and remain progressively dominated by the central gravity well. The motion is sufficiently consequential to justify testing whether a temporary density and heat field can reconstruct it as continuous or semi-continuous molten material.

Do not discard the diagnostic parcel view. It should remain available while developing reconstruction so visual styling cannot conceal a dynamics regression.

## Implementation location

The parcel study is a sibling experiment at:

    src/casting-pit/parcels/

The v0.2 Bézier-ribbon implementation in `src/casting-pit/ribbons/` remains unchanged as experimental evidence.

## Implemented model

The experiment simulates a maximum of `500` CPU-side parcels in the membrane's material coordinates `(x,z)`. Each parcel carries:

- position;
- velocity;
- effective mass;
- temperature;
- age and lifetime;
- source identity;
- temporary acceleration accumulated during a simulation step.

World-space height is never integrated independently. It is evaluated from the same `membraneHeight(x,z)` function used by the v0.1 membrane whenever diagnostic geometry is updated.

The simulation uses semi-implicit Euler integration at a fixed `1/60` second. A frame-time accumulator separates display rate from dynamics and permits at most three catch-up steps before dropping excess accumulated time after a stalled frame.

For each step:

1. Six source regions emit parcels with different positions, directions, speeds, cadences and masses.
2. The analytic gradient of the existing Gaussian membrane supplies downhill acceleration.
3. Existing velocity preserves momentum.
4. Exponential drag moderates that momentum.
5. A uniform spatial hash limits neighbour searches to adjacent cells.
6. Nearby parcels apply three local effects: short-range pressure/separation, velocity alignment/viscosity and mild cohesion.
7. Velocity and position are integrated, with a conservative speed cap for numerical stability.
8. Parcels are recycled at the receiver, membrane escape, excessive age or low temperature.

No Bézier curve, spline, waypoint, steering noise or hidden trajectory is used.

## Sources and encounters

The first source arrangement produced attractive curved motion but failed the central interaction test: despite many same-stream neighbour contacts, a sampled step after nine seconds contained `0` cross-source pairs. Gravity redirected the populations before their intended geometric intersections.

The final arrangement uses three deliberately opposed pairs of initial velocity fields aimed toward off-centre encounter regions. These are initial conditions rather than paths. The membrane gradient, momentum, drag and neighbour forces determine what happens after emission.

A deterministic seeded random generator was added so resets reproduce parcel position, velocity, mass, temperature and cadence variation. This permits meaningful interaction-on/off comparisons rather than comparing unrelated random runs.

## Interaction findings

The original `0.52` world-unit neighbour radius was smaller than the effective spacing and width of the parcel trains. Cross-source contact occurred only intermittently, even at interaction strength `2.0`.

Increasing the radius to `0.78`, modestly strengthening velocity alignment and using a default aggregate interaction strength of `1.4` produced sustained cross-population contact without instability.

In repeatable comparison runs:

- With interaction disabled, populations crossed encounter regions rapidly and remained loose independent dots. Sampled cross-source proximity was commonly around `13–16` pairs.
- With interaction `1.4`, the same seeded initial conditions produced roughly `174` cross-source pairs in a representative sampled step.
- Interacting populations formed tighter trains, remained in mixed-population proximity longer and showed local entrainment and temporary accumulation before the well carried them onward.
- Unequal source mass and momentum caused asymmetrical encounters rather than mirrored collision outcomes.

The experiment does not hard-merge parcel identities. Joining is expressed as sustained proximity and velocity entrainment, which is the appropriate input for density-field reconstruction.

## Useful parameter ranges

- Emission rate: approximately `36–60` parcels per second. The default `48` makes encounters legible while normally keeping the active population below the `500` cap. Higher rates saturate the budget and make individual consequences harder to inspect.
- Gravity influence: approximately `2.5–4.2`. The default `3.2` preserves visible launch momentum on the outer field while making the central slope progressively dominant.
- Drag: approximately `0.20–0.45`. The default `0.32` retains consequential momentum without permitting sustained orbiting or escape.
- Interaction strength: approximately `1.1–1.7` with the final `0.78` neighbour radius. The default `1.4` gives sustained entrainment without explosive separation or rigid clumping.
- The v0.1 surface defaults—depth `5.5`, width `3.8`—remain balanced. Depth `6.5` and width `4.4` were also tested successfully for attachment and stability.

Boundary observations:

- Gravity `1.2` with drag `0.12` produces long ballistic approaches and heavy central accumulation. It still converges, but source momentum dominates for too much of the journey.
- Gravity `5.5` with drag `0.65` produces orderly, compressed source trains and delays encounters; the movement begins to look prescribed even though it is not.
- Interaction `0` is a useful diagnostic control because it exposes how readily independent parcels pass through the same region without consequence.

## Smooth membrane finding

The smooth membrane is sufficient for this stage.

Variation comes from source position, launch velocity, cadence, mass, persistent momentum and neighbour interaction. No visible rough terrain, stochastic steering force or arbitrary noise was required. The outer field allows different momentum histories to develop; the increasing Gaussian slope then establishes dominant inward convergence.

This does not prove that small-scale structure can never improve the final material. It shows that adding it is not necessary to obtain consequential dynamics and therefore should not be introduced before molten reconstruction is assessed.

## Diagnostic rendering and attachment

Parcels are deliberately plain source-coloured points. Optional velocity line segments display their current material-space velocity projected back onto the membrane. The source colours make mixed populations and encounter regions inspectable without suggesting finished lava.

The existing membrane wireframe can be enabled independently. At depth `6.5` and width `4.4`, both parcel points and velocity vectors remained attached to the changed surface. There was no floating, clipping or separate height state.

The monumental and elevated camera presets, manual orbit/zoom/pan, surface lighting, exposure, resize handling and device-pixel-ratio cap remain available.

## Performance

Observed active counts were generally about `340–470` parcels at the default emission rate, depending on elapsed time and removal at the receiver.

Representative fixed-step timings were approximately `0.4–3.1 ms`, including source emission, analytic surface forces, spatial-hash construction, neighbour interaction and integration. The enlarged interaction neighbourhood produced roughly `1,400–3,900` same- or cross-source neighbour pairs in sampled steps while remaining responsive.

The browser reported no runtime warnings or errors. The experiment remains comfortably interactive on the test machine, although reconstruction will add a separate field-generation and texture-upload budget.

## Successful behaviours

- Trajectories arise from state and force rather than authored paths.
- Initial momentum remains visible across the calm outer membrane.
- The central slope progressively curves and accelerates motion inward.
- Different source populations enter common neighbourhoods.
- Interaction changes dwell time, coherence and local velocity compared with a seeded zero-interaction run.
- Pressure prevents immediate point collapse.
- Viscosity/alignment and cohesion create entrainment and temporary accumulation.
- Motion does not become random spray.
- Motion does not form an accretion-disc orbit.
- Parcels remain surface-bound during live deformation.
- The receiver reliably removes converged parcels rather than allowing an unresolved central pile to grow indefinitely.

## Failure modes and limitations

- The first source arrangement proved that visually crossing projected lines do not guarantee cross-population neighbour interaction on the curved surface.
- A small neighbour radius allowed streams to ignore one another; an effective radius must correspond to the intended reconstructed stream width.
- Strong gravity combined with strong drag suppresses momentum history and creates overly orderly source trains.
- Low gravity and low drag can make the outer journey too ballistic and move most accumulation to the receiver.
- The active count can reach the `500` cap. When full, new emissions are skipped rather than queued; v0.2b-2 should avoid treating this as a meaningful change in source volume.
- The cross-source counter measures proximity during a step, not a conserved quantity or a formal merge event. Its value is diagnostic evidence and can vary rapidly.
- Point trains still look like particles by design. This study says nothing yet about whether density thresholding, persistence and heat rendering will yield convincing molten continuity.
- The current local interaction model is an artistic approximation. It does not conserve a full fluid pressure field or model incompressible flow.
- Large live changes to depth or width preserve attachment but instantaneously change acceleration; velocity is not reprojected or eased across that edit.

## Changes from the reconnaissance proposal

- The catch-up cap is three fixed steps rather than the suggested normal budget of two. This tolerates minor frame jitter while still discarding a large backlog after a stalled tab.
- Deterministic seeded resets were added after the first visual comparison showed that uncontrolled random variation obscured the effect of interaction.
- Cross-source neighbour contacts are reported separately from all neighbour pairs after the original counter proved misleading.
- The neighbour radius increased from `0.52` to `0.78` only after evidence showed that the smaller radius produced too little consequential contact.
- Three paired encounter regions were encoded through source positions and initial velocities. No collision result or subsequent route is encoded.

## Implication for v0.2b-2

The next experiment should reconstruct these parcels through a temporary material-space density and heat field, as proposed in the v0.2b reconnaissance. It should preserve the deterministic reset and plain parcel diagnostic as a comparison mode.

The reconstruction study must determine whether entrained parcels become visibly joined material, whether short-lived accumulation reads as volume rather than glow, and whether field persistence bridges parcel spacing without painting permanent trails.

Do not interpret recommendation A as authorization to begin reconstruction in this session. No v0.2b-2 implementation was started.
