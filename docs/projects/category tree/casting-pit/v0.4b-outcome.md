# Casting Pit v0.4b outcome — Hierarchical Cosmos

14 September 2026

## Principal question

Can ordered mathematical structure become locally intricate and metropolitan at several scales without allowing the Casting Pit to become its organising centre?

**Yes, provisionally.** v0.4b replaces the ring-first grammar with an explicit multi-centre hierarchy and preserves the living graphite, gold and ember substrate. A propagation review also identified and corrected a significant distinction between constant logical speed and constant speed through the spatially manifested network.

## Experiment

The independent study is located at:

    src/casting-pit/cosmic-circuitry-v0.4b/

It is a sibling of v0.4a, which remains unchanged. No lava, gates, reservoirs, throat, Tree, Category Tree semantics, post-processing or new physical simulation was introduced.

## Generative approach

The logical Cosmos occupies a two-dimensional territory larger than the visible membrane. District candidates are jittered within a `5 × 5` stratification and selected farthest-first, distributing local centres across the territory without reference to the Casting Pit. A minimum-spanning structure connects them into one coherent graph, locality-preserving cross-links introduce large-scale alternatives, and boundary conduits continue beyond the observable surface.

Each district receives its own orientation and activity. Principal distribution axes create local conditions for recursive continuation and near-perpendicular lateral division. Successive generations become shorter and visually finer. Local closures are permitted only between nearby nodes whose relation agrees with the district axes. This reduces arbitrary triangular noise while producing subsidiary circuitry at several scales.

The calibrated default seed `2` produces approximately 3,059 nodes and 4,222 connections. Density varies by district, leaving both intricate territories and sparse regions. At maximum arterial connectivity, district density and subdivision depth, the tested graph reached approximately 3,955 nodes and 5,448 connections.

## Hierarchy

Hierarchy is carried by both topology and manifestation:

- major conduits connect districts and visible boundaries;
- intermediate distribution axes establish distinct local organisations;
- recursive divisions form subsidiary networks;
- axis-compatible closures provide fine connectivity;
- length, width and luminosity decrease with structural tier;
- varying district activity creates several local scales of density.

The result is not a literal city, building plan or circuit board. Its useful analogy is a small aerial sample of an enormously larger computational metropolis.

## Independence from the gravity well

Network generation never reads membrane height, slope, depression controls, distance from the origin or gravity. The graph's logical nodes, adjacency and routes are complete before they are spatially manifested. The origin has no privileged topological role.

Only `projectedCoordinates()` knows about the membrane. `Surface binding` interpolates rendered height between a flat plane and the deformed surface. Changing this value alters the spatial appearance and length of conduits, but not what connects to what or which route a signal follows.

## Final propagation model

Information follows topology independent of the Casting Pit but travels at essentially constant speed through the spatially manifested route geometry.

Each communication route stores its graph nodes and edge identities. Every edge also stores the four-point rough logical polyline used to render its visible centre path. For a route, those edge polylines are oriented in the direction of traversal, projected onto the current surface, joined without duplicate junction points, and accumulated using Euclidean 3D distance.

A signal advances through this manifested cumulative length by:

    manifested distance += communication speed × elapsed time

All signals use a speed factor of exactly `1`. Position is interpolated between the two manifested samples surrounding the current distance. Gravity, slope and direction never enter the propagation equation.

When depth, width, territory sampling or surface binding changes, manifested route lengths are rebuilt. Each active signal retains its fractional progress through its route while its absolute distance is remapped to the new geometry.

## Why the correction was required

The original v0.4b code advanced through cumulative logical 2D distance and projected only the resulting point. This guaranteed constant speed in the undeformed graph but not in shared 3D space. On a slope, equal logical increments correspond to greater manifested distance, so packets moved physically faster in both descending and ascending directions.

At the default depth `5.5`, width `3.8` and surface binding `0.82`, the maximum theoretical local stretch for a radially aligned segment is approximately `1.43×`. At full binding it is approximately `1.59×`. Under the diagnostic state of depth `8` and full binding, it can exceed `2×`. The discrepancy was therefore conceptually and visually meaningful rather than negligible.

## Visual and technical findings

- The ring, astrolabe and globally centred grammar has been removed.
- Major routes can cross, avoid or pass through the well without being generated by it.
- Multiple district orientations make local order visible at several scales.
- The graphite shoulders, ember cores and gold seams retain the living-substrate character established in v0.4a.
- White-hot information droplets with amber aureoles and directional discharge tails travel through actual graph adjacency and remain visually distinct from persistent substrate heat.
- The network remains judgeable with communication density reduced to zero.
- With manifested arc-length propagation, packets no longer acquire extra spatial speed on steep descent or ascent.
- Signals now follow the same rough sampled centre path as the rendered conduit more accurately than the original node-to-node interpolation.
- Browser inspection at default and strong deformation produced no console warnings or errors.
- The production build succeeds. Vite reports only the existing advisory that the Three.js bundle exceeds 500 kB.

## Useful control ranges

- Arterial connectivity `0.00–1.00` progressively adds large-scale alternatives; the default is `0.45`.
- District density `0.00–1.00` selects 8–25 districts; the default is `0.60`, representing 18 districts.
- Subdivision depth `3–7` provides a broad simplification-to-saturation range; the default is `7`.
- District footprint `0.75–2.25` changes local reach while preserving local topology; the default is `1.70`.
- Territory sample X/Z can move intact dense districts onto useful surrounding terrain without changing topology. Values within approximately `-4–4` retain broad coverage in the current camera views.
- Surface binding `0.70–1.00` is useful for inspecting the shared physical manifestation; `0` remains the topology diagnostic.
- Communication speed `2–5` makes constant travel easy to follow while retaining informational character; the default is `2.50`.
- Communication density defaults to `110` and brightness to `0.85`. Density `70–140` remains useful when balancing individual droplets against metropolitan activity.

## Performance implications

Topology generation remains the dominant occasional cost. The correction adds a manifested polyline and cumulative-distance array to each of 140 routes. These are rebuilt only when network or manifestation geometry changes.

Per-frame signal lookup now traverses more samples because each graph edge contributes three rendered subsegments rather than one node-to-node segment. The current route lengths and signal ceiling remained responsive in browser testing, including maximum deformation and density. If future studies introduce substantially longer routes or many more signals, binary search over cumulative distance would be a straightforward optimisation.

## Remaining limitations

- Districts can still read as branching filaments at low subdivision depth.
- Maximum structural settings intentionally approach luminous urban saturation and can collapse hierarchy into line noise; this is now useful exploratory headroom rather than the only convincing state.
- Some minimum-spanning connections retain a graph-theoretic appearance.
- Visual crossings are not junctions unless they share an explicit graph node.
- Ember motion is a global material pulse rather than locally circulating heat.
- Manifested length is measured along the rendered four-point edge polyline rather than an analytic continuous surface curve. This is faithful to current rendering but may require denser sampling under future extreme deformation.
- The isolated study does not establish how well the Cosmos will survive occlusion, colour competition or compositional pressure from the molten Universe.

## Communication visibility refinement

The first manifested-distance build retained communication brightness `0.3` and a one-pixel discharge head. Human review correctly found that packets had become artistically unreadable against the brighter living circuitry. The propagation model was sound; its visual encoding was not.

Communication now combines the existing short discharge tail with two point-sprite layers at the packet head: a compact white-hot core and a broader amber halo. A procedural radial canvas texture supplies a soft luminous falloff without a new dependency or external asset. Both layers use the same manifested route position, so the droplet cannot detach from the graph and does not alter routing or speed.

The revised default brightness is `1.25`, with a core diameter of approximately `0.23` world units and a halo diameter of approximately `0.52` world units. Browser inspection in Elevated and Monumental views confirmed that packets are immediately recognisable as moving luminous droplets while the fading tail continues to indicate direction. This restores communication as an essential visible behaviour rather than a detail that must be inferred.

## Surrounding-terrain distribution refinement

Pre-layering review showed that the original global `Circuitry scale` control was compositionally and conceptually unhelpful. Because it scaled coordinates around `(0,0)`, reducing it concentrated complexity toward the well; increasing it spread a fixed graph apart and reduced apparent detail. This made valuable fine structure likely to be hidden later by lava and the Tree.

The generator now uses stratified territorial district placement. Twenty-five independently jittered cells supply candidates, and farthest-first selection chooses the number required by district density. This gives the logical Cosmos broad coverage before it knows anything about membrane manifestation. It does not create a protected ring around the pit, repel nodes from the centre or otherwise allow the Casting Pit to determine topology.

Global scale has been replaced with `District footprint`, which affects the reach of each local hierarchy. Independent `Territory sample X/Z` controls translate the generated graph only when manifested. They function as movement of the observation window through a much larger Cosmos: topology, adjacency, route choice and subdivision count remain fixed, while the part encountered by the local physical event changes.

The final defaults use district density `0.50` on the expanded 25-site field and subdivision depth `6`. This makes fine subsidiary networks visible across the surrounding terrain rather than relying on detail concentrated inside the well. A live X `4`, Z `-3` test relocated intact dense districts across the plateaux without packet discontinuity or route change. The correction therefore improves future overlay resilience while preserving the principle that the visible scene samples the Cosmos rather than being composed by the pit.

## Interactive visual calibration

Human review established that the previous maxima for district density, subdivision depth and footprint produced a substantially more compelling artistic impression than the former default. Rather than merely promoting those ceilings, the control model was expanded and made deterministic enough to behave as a consistent exploratory instrument.

The former `Arterial density` was not monotonic because its random decisions advanced the same generator later used for district growth. A higher value could therefore replace dense local structures with a sparser random outcome. It is now `Arterial connectivity`, with independent seeded random streams for arterial links and boundary continuations. Each district also owns deterministic growth and closure streams, while communication route choice has its own stream. Changing one structural family consequently no longer re-rolls unrelated families.

District density now selects between 8 and 25 farthest-first district sites and does not also alter local branching probability. Subdivision depth alone controls recursive generation. District footprint expands or contracts local geometry, with the closure radius scaled proportionally so topology remains unchanged across its range.

The recalibrated default is deliberately close to the previously discovered compelling state: depression depth `7.1`, width `3.7`, circuitry intensity `0.65`, arterial connectivity `0.45`, 18 districts, subdivision depth `7`, footprint `1.70`, territory Z `4`, and 110 communication droplets at speed `2.50` and brightness `0.85`. It produces approximately 3,059 nodes and 4,222 connections and opens in the Elevated view. The controls retain useful simplifying travel and additional density/footprint headroom; the expanded maximum of 25 districts, depth `7` and footprint `2.25` produced approximately 3,955 nodes and 5,448 connections without browser errors or loss of responsiveness.

Foreground review exposed an additional compositional issue: the Monumental camera clipped much of the near subsidiary structure, so the default foreground read as a few broad arteries despite considerable total complexity. A live comparison showed that simply adding density was an inefficient remedy. The final default instead combines the Elevated camera with territory Z `4`, sampling an intact denser part of the logical Cosmos across the foreground and near rim. The well remains legible, and the solution preserves topology independence because it changes only camera composition and manifestation offset.

## Suitability for a future overlay

The experiment is technically suitable for a bounded Universe/Cosmos overlay. Its topology is independent, its manifestation belongs to the same surface, and its communication now maintains constant speed in that manifested reality. An overlay remains necessary to judge visual coexistence; this outcome does not assume that the two systems will compose successfully without adjustment.

## Recommendation

**B. Preserve v0.4b and proceed to a bounded Universe/Cosmos overlay experiment.**

The propagation concern exposed a genuine defect, but the correction fits the conceptual model cleanly and required no compromise to graph independence. No further isolated propagation redesign is indicated before a controlled overlay test.
