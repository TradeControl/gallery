# Casting Pit v0.4b review — Hierarchical Cosmos

14 September 2026

## Review status

This is the mandatory review checkpoint for v0.4b. It records the implemented experiment and questions for visual inspection. It is deliberately not the final v0.4b outcome or a recommendation about the next experiment.

The implementation is in `src/casting-pit/cosmic-circuitry-v0.4b/`. The v0.4a sibling remains unchanged.

## Implemented generative grammar

The ring-first generator has been removed completely.

The logical network is generated over a square territory larger than the visible membrane. It begins with district candidates jittered within a `5 × 5` stratification. Farthest-first selection produces broad territorial coverage without querying the well or imposing a radial exclusion. A minimum-spanning graph joins the selected centres into one coherent system; a limited number of locality-preserving cross-links add large-scale loops. Additional major conduits connect boundary points outside the visible surface to nearby districts, allowing structures to enter and leave the observation window.

Each district has its own randomly seeded orientation and activity. From its centre, two principal distribution directions establish a local axis; more active districts may also receive transverse routes. Those structures recursively continue and occasionally divide laterally at near-perpendicular angles. Successive generations become shorter, narrower and less luminous. Nearby local nodes may close into circuits only when their relationship agrees with the district's axes, avoiding arbitrary triangular cross-link noise.

The result is one explicit graph with adjacency, not a collection of unrelated marks.

## Independence from the Casting Pit

All nodes and connections are generated in a logical two-dimensional coordinate system. The generator never reads membrane height, depression depth, depression width, distance from `(0,0)`, slope or gravity. The origin has no special topological status.

District placement, arterial relationships, recursive headings and local closure are complete before spatial manifestation. Routes can therefore cross the well, pass beside it or remain outside it solely because the independently generated graph occupies those positions.

The selected default is deterministic network seed `2`. Reseeding demonstrates that major junctions and dense territories move independently while the gravity well remains fixed.

## Manifestation on the shared surface

After topology exists, each logical node is projected into the scene. `Surface binding` interpolates only its rendered height between a flat mathematical plane and the deformed membrane. It does not change adjacency, logical position, edge length or communication routing.

The retained graphite/gold/ember treatment represents each edge with dark incised shoulders, a persistent variable-temperature ember core and a fine gold seam. Edge tier controls width and luminosity so that major, intermediate and fine structures remain part of one material system.

## Representation of hierarchy

Hierarchy is encoded in both topology and rendering:

- major conduits connect districts and continue beyond the visible territory;
- district axes establish local orientation and intermediate distribution;
- recursive continuations and lateral divisions create smaller subsidiary structures;
- axis-compatible local closures add fine connectivity without arbitrary graph noise;
- each generation reduces physical reach and visual weight;
- district activity varies, producing dense local organisations and sparse territories.

This produces order at several scales from relationships inherited through generation rather than by adding positional noise to one repeated primitive.

## Information propagation

Communication remains graph-routed, but propagation is now parameterised by manifested three-dimensional route distance rather than logical two-dimensional distance. Each signal advances by `communication speed × elapsed time` with a speed factor of exactly `1`.

Every graph edge retains the same logical rough polyline used to render its channel. After surface projection, those points are assembled in route order and measured cumulatively in 3D. Signal interpolation uses this manifested polyline and its cumulative lengths. A longer descent therefore takes proportionally longer to traverse than its shorter planar projection, producing constant spatial speed rather than gravity-like acceleration. Delayed starts and differing initial route positions prevent global synchronisation without altering propagation speed.

Topology remains independent: the membrane changes neither adjacency nor route choice. It changes only the spatial embedding and therefore the physical length through which information must propagate.

## Controls most useful for review

- **Arterial connectivity** (`0.00–1.00`, default `0.45`) progressively adds large-scale cross-links and boundary continuations without re-rolling local districts.
- **District density** (`0.00–1.00`, default `0.60`) selects between 8 and 25 territorial districts. It no longer also changes local branching probability.
- **Subdivision depth** (`3–7`, default `7`) controls recursive intricacy.
- **District footprint** (`0.75–2.25`, default `1.70`) changes local branch reach without changing its node or closure topology.
- **Territory sample X/Z** (`-8–8`, default `0`) translate the intact logical Cosmos across the fixed Casting Pit so that different parts of the greater network can occupy the observation window.
- **Surface binding** (`0–1`, default `0.82`) separates topology from manifestation for inspection.
- **Circuitry intensity** (`0.05–1.50`, default `0.55`) controls the living substrate without changing its graph.
- **Communication density**, **speed** and **brightness** retain their v0.4a roles. Density can be reduced to `0` to judge the static topology honestly.
- Communication brightness now defaults to `1.25` after the propagation follow-up revealed that thin discharges at `0.3` were being lost against the living ember substrate.

## Suggested review states

1. **Default structural judgement:** seed `2`, Elevated camera, arterial `0.42`, district `0.85`, subdivision `6`, district footprint `1`, territory X/Z `0`, binding `0.82`, communication density `70`.
2. **Topology without assistance from motion:** the default state with communication density `0`.
3. **Independence diagnostic:** communication density `0` and surface binding `0`; the network becomes planar without changing its connectivity or relation to the fixed well.
4. **Sparse hierarchy:** arterial approximately `0.25`, district approximately `0.45`, subdivision `3`.
5. **Stress state:** arterial `1`, district `1`, subdivision `7`, footprint `2.25`. The calibrated seed `2` produced approximately 3,955 nodes and 5,448 edges and remained responsive in browser review.
6. **Generative independence:** return to defaults and use `Reseed network` several times while observing that no stable centre, ring or radial relationship to the well persists.
7. **Propagation stress test:** seed `2`, Monumental camera, surface binding `1`, depression depth `8`, communication density `140`, speed `2`, and brightness approximately `1.2`. Follow individual white-gold strokes across level routes, down the near and far slopes, through the lower region and back upward.

Both Elevated and Monumental cameras should be inspected. Elevated explains the multi-scale topology most clearly; Monumental is more useful for judging whether the network convincingly inhabits the membrane.

## Known visual weaknesses

- Some district growth can still read as branching filaments rather than a fully metropolitan fabric, especially at subdivision depths below `4`.
- At maximum settings, overlapping districts approach line noise and can lose the intended distinction between arterial, distribution and fine scales.
- The minimum-spanning backbone can occasionally produce conspicuous long connections. Local cross-links reduce but do not eliminate that graph-theoretic reading.
- Near-perpendicular local inheritance introduces computational order, but some seeds may feel too rectilinear while others retain an organic or nervous-system character.
- Edge crossings are not automatically converted into junctions. A visual crossing connects only when both routes share an explicit graph node.
- The living ember animation remains a restrained global material pulse; it does not yet model independent local heat circulation.
- Junction markers remain deliberately subtle and may not make every topological distinction legible at a distant camera.

## Differences from the brief

No scoped visual layer was added and no excluded Casting Pit or Tree system was touched.

The hierarchy begins by sampling district centres and then building major structure between them; it is therefore not a strict one-way grammar in which arteries are always generated first and districts only grow afterward. Large and local organisation are nevertheless causally coupled: district centres determine the coherent arterial graph, and each centre then establishes inherited axes for its subsidiary network. Whether this satisfies the intended sense that large structure creates conditions for smaller structure is a visual and conceptual review question.

No screenshots have been committed. The deterministic states above are preferable because motion, projection and camera parallax are important to the judgement.

## Questions for review

1. Does the default first impression escape concentric rings, spider webs and astrolabes?
2. Does any part of the graph still appear implicitly organised by the well despite the topology's mathematical independence?
3. Are major conduits, districts, subsidiary structures and fine connections all perceptible as distinct scales?
4. Do axis-inherited local structures feel metropolitan and computational, or merely like rectilinear branches?
5. Is the contrast between dense districts and sparse territory convincing at seed `2` and across reseeds?
6. Does the topology remain compelling with communication density at `0`?
7. Are the ember substrate and white-gold information discharges still sufficiently distinct while belonging to one luminous reality?
8. Does the network feel like a bounded generated diagram, or a small view onto a much larger Cosmos?
9. At what control settings does intricacy become noise rather than hierarchy?
10. Is this generative grammar worth refining, or is its district-and-artery premise itself conceptually insufficient?

## Propagation review amendment

The follow-up challenge was valid. The original v0.4b implementation accumulated logical 2D edge length and advanced a signal through that metric before projecting the interpolated point. Its statement that rendering-only projection guaranteed constant visible speed was incorrect. Projection preserved route and direction, but not arc length.

For the default membrane, the maximum radial surface gradient is approximately `1.24`. At the default binding of `0.82`, this can stretch a suitably aligned manifested segment by approximately `1.43×`; at full binding the maximum is approximately `1.59×`. The original signal could therefore cover about 43% more manifested distance per second on a steep default-bound slope than on level ground. With depth `8`, width `3.8` and full binding, the local ratio can exceed `2×`. This is significant enough to undermine the conceptual distinction between routed information and falling matter.

Each generated route now stores both logical metrics and a manifested polyline. The manifested polyline is built from the exact rough edge samples used by the visible conduit, oriented according to route traversal, with duplicate junction samples removed. Cumulative Euclidean distance is then calculated through those projected 3D points. Packet position and trail length are evaluated against that metric.

Manifested metrics are rebuilt when depression depth, depression width, territory sampling or surface binding changes. Existing signal distance is remapped by its fractional route progress so control changes do not restart or arbitrarily relocate traffic. Network generation, adjacency, route choice and logical positions are untouched.

Live inspection used both camera views at the default binding and a strong-deformation state of binding `1`, depth `8`, 140 signals, speed `2` and brightness `1.2`. Signals remained attached to the visible rough conduits and showed no systematic speed increase on descent or ascent. Lower and level sections used the same world-space propagation rate. The stronger brightness and density were diagnostic aids only; defaults remain unchanged. No browser warnings or errors were reported.

The remaining limitation is geometric sampling. Manifested arc length follows each conduit's four-point rough polyline per graph edge, not the analytic continuous heightfield curve. This matches the rendered centre path and is adequate at the present edge subdivision. If future geometry uses longer edges or substantially sharper surface deformation, adaptive sampling or a denser fixed subdivision may be warranted.

## Communication visibility amendment

Human review of the corrected propagation build showed that communication had become too difficult to distinguish from the persistent circuitry. The speed correction did not reduce opacity, but v0.4b spreads the same event count across a larger network, while the substrate now glows more strongly than when brightness `0.3` was first chosen. A one-pixel pale discharge at that setting merged into the conductive seam.

Each signal now has three coordinated parts: a compact white-hot droplet core, a larger soft amber aureole and the existing fading directional discharge tail. Both point layers share the exact manifested head position used by the trail. They therefore remain inside the conduit and inherit the corrected constant-distance propagation rather than becoming a second particle system.

The droplet texture is generated locally with a radial canvas gradient; no asset or dependency was introduced. Core and halo use additive blending with world-space size attenuation. Communication brightness controls all three components and now defaults to `1.25`. Browser inspection at defaults confirmed that individual events are immediately visible in Elevated and Monumental views without obscuring the network. No browser warnings or errors were reported.

## Surrounding-terrain distribution amendment

Layering review identified a compositional weakness: high subdivision could still place much of the most useful fine structure on the slopes and inside the well, where future lava and Tree geometry would obscure it. The old `Circuitry scale` control made this worse because it multiplied all coordinates around `(0,0)`; values below `1` literally compressed the visible Cosmos towards the pit, while values above `1` reduced apparent complexity by spreading existing nodes apart.

Global circuitry scaling has been removed. District centres now come from a jittered stratified logical territory and are selected for mutual separation. This distributes opportunities for intricate local organisation across the observation window without measuring distance from the well or excluding its centre. Subdivision depth defaults to `6`; a recalibrated district density of `0.50` now selects 17 of 25 possible districts, making the subsidiary fabric visible across more of the surrounding terrain while retaining meaningful headroom.

Three formerly entangled judgements are now separate. `District footprint` changes local branch reach and regenerates the same kind of hierarchical graph without globally contracting it. `Subdivision depth` controls recursive intricacy. `Territory sample X` and `Territory sample Z` translate the complete generated network during manifestation only, allowing a different part of the independent Cosmos to lie beneath the fixed Casting Pit.

At the calibrated default seed and controls, browser inspection produced approximately 3,059 nodes and 4,222 connections. Setting territory X to `4` and Z to `-3` visibly moved intact dense districts onto different surrounding plateaux while preserving their local structure and the corrected packet propagation. The expanded maximum structural state produced approximately 3,955 nodes and 5,448 connections and remained responsive. No browser warnings or errors were reported.

## Interactive calibration amendment

Human review found that the most compelling information layer required the previous maximum district density, subdivision depth and footprint, plus manual camera rotation. The issue was not simply conservative defaults: the controls did not form a reliable artistic instrument. `Arterial density` in particular consumed the same seeded random sequence as later district growth, so increasing it could re-roll the local network and appear to reduce density.

The generator now uses independent deterministic streams for arterial cross-links, boundary continuations, each district's recursive growth, each district's closures and communication routes. Existing local districts therefore retain their identity while arterial connectivity changes. District density now controls district count only; subdivision depth controls local recursive generations; footprint scales local reach while its closure-distance test scales with it. Endpoint testing confirmed monotonic node counts: district density moved from approximately 985 to 2,369 nodes at depth `6`, subdivision depth moved from approximately 436 to 2,902 nodes at central density, and arterial connectivity moved from approximately 1,704 to 1,776 nodes without replacing the district fabric. Footprint `0.75–2.25` preserved an identical node and edge count at a fixed density and depth.

The high-impact state now anchors the default: depth `7.1`, width `3.7`, circuitry intensity `0.65`, district density `0.60` of the expanded 25-site field, subdivision depth `7`, footprint `1.70`, territory Z `4`, communication density `110`, speed `2.50` and brightness `0.85`. The Elevated camera is the initial view. This gives a legible metropolitan field immediately while preserving substantial simplifying travel and further density/footprint headroom.

Subsequent foreground review showed that the lower Monumental default hid much of the available subsidiary fabric beneath the near frame edge, leaving the foreground dependent on a few broad arteries. Testing territory sampling and camera elevation found that moving the manifested Cosmos toward the viewer with territory Z `4`, combined with the Elevated view, carries intricate districts across both foreground shoulders and the near rim. This is a compositional sampling choice only: no foreground attractor, pit-aware density rule or change to logical topology was introduced.
