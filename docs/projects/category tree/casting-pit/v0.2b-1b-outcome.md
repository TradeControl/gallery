# Casting Pit v0.2b-1b outcome — Variable Packet Streams

6 September 2026

## Scope completed

The Casting Pit studies now sit beneath one project directory:

    src/casting-pit/
    ├── ribbons/
    ├── parcels/
    └── variable-packets/

`ribbons/` contains the prescribed Bézier/ribbon study unchanged. `parcels/` contains the v0.2b-1a Hostile Marbles diagnostic unchanged. `variable-packets/` is a separate derivative, so the successful adversarial test harness remains available as evidence.

No changes were made to `src/getting-started/`.

## Source generation

The new experiment defaults to **6 streams** and exposes an integer **Number of streams** range of **2–12**.

For a given seed and stream count, source generation is deterministic. A small seeded xorshift generator creates the complete source arrangement once, while a separate reproducible simulation random stream supplies the existing per-packet jitter. **Reset parcels** replays the same arrangement and packet sequence. **Reseed** advances the displayed integer seed and creates a new deterministic arrangement; it does not randomise during a run.

Sources occupy an annulus between radii `7.2` and `10.4`. The annulus is divided into angular sectors for coverage, but each position receives a bounded angular offset of up to 55% of its sector width and an independently varied radius. The result avoids both clustering everything on one side and producing evenly spaced radial spokes.

Each heading is defined relative to its local inward radial direction. The generator guarantees:

- one substantially inward heading, within approximately ±0.22 radians of inward;
- one substantially tangential heading, approximately 1.38–1.62 radians from inward;
- one oblique heading when at least three sources exist, approximately 0.55–1.05 radians from inward;
- one heading with an outward component when at least four sources exist, approximately 2.05–2.65 radians from inward;
- bounded oblique variation for remaining sources, within approximately ±1.25 radians of inward.

Initial speed varies from `1.25` to `2.15`, mass from `0.85` to `1.20`, cadence weight from `0.82` to `1.18`, and starting radius as described above. These ranges distinguish streams without introducing adversarial extremes.

Only initial conditions are generated. No source is selected to intercept another, and there are no paths, waypoints, steering or source-specific corrective forces after launch. Short coloured ticks show position and initial direction without participating in the dynamics.

## Constant total input

Raw cadence weights are normalised so their sum equals the stream count. Each source emits at:

    total emission rate × normalised cadence weight ÷ stream count

Consequently, the sum across every generated source is the displayed overall emission rate: `48` packets per second by default. Moving from 2 to 12 streams changes how that fixed input is fragmented, not its total volume.

## Dynamics

The v0.2b-1a dynamics were carried forward without coefficient or algorithm changes. The membrane, gradient force, momentum, drag, pressure/separation, viscosity/alignment, cohesion, spatial hash, fixed step, receiver, lifetime and surface attachment are unchanged.

The only additions are source generation, source markers, dynamic colour/key presentation, stream-count reset and reseeding.

## Visual observations

Tests used the default gravity `3.2`, drag `0.32`, interaction `1.4` and total emission `48`, with stream counts 2, 6 and 12 and source seeds 1 and 2.

### Two streams

The input appears as two substantial trains rather than many thin traces. With seed 1, the inward stream captured strongly (660 captures after approximately 26 seconds), while the tangential stream resisted the well and ultimately escaped (173 losses). With only two widely separated sources there were no cross-source neighbour interactions in the sampled frame.

The parcel pool approached its 500 limit because the failed tangential population remained active until its lifetime or boundary test resolved. This is an intelligible asymmetric history, not universal immediate capture.

### Six streams

At seed 1, four populations were already capturing after approximately 12 seconds while the tangential and outward cases remained unresolved. The scene showed several distinct curved histories and local accumulations rather than six radial spokes.

At seed 2, five populations captured and the generated outward-component stream failed: after approximately 24 seconds it had 25 losses and no captures, while the other streams had 139–180 captures each. This confirms that convergence is not dependent on one fortunate arrangement and that generated failures remain possible.

### Twelve streams

At seed 1, the same total input was visibly divided into thinner and more fragmented activity. Cross-source contacts became common near the central depression. After approximately 27 seconds ten populations were capturing; the tangential population showed both capture and escape (62 / 14), while the outward population escaped (0 / 35). The sampled frame contained 844 cross-source neighbour pairs.

At seed 2, all twelve populations converged during the observation period, with 54–86 captures per source after approximately 24 seconds and 1,177 sampled cross-source neighbour pairs. The different outcome from seed 1 demonstrates that source geometry matters without making the system generally dependent on hand-authored launches.

## Pooling and convergence

Temporary packet accumulations were visible on the outer slope, at some crossing regions and around the lower depression. Their location and persistence changed with stream count and seed. Some were queues caused by a long-lived unsuccessful stream; others dispersed or drained as interactions and the steeper inner gradient took effect.

The diagnostic cannot yet decide whether these become convincing molten pools or visible particle congestion after reconstruction. They were therefore preserved. No tuning was added to suppress them.

The outer membrane remains comparatively free-flowing: initial direction and momentum can carry packets across, around or away from the well. For appropriate trajectories the steeper inner gradient increasingly bends the paths and dominates near the receiver. Capture, escape, crossing and accumulation all occurred without choreography.

## Performance

The 500-parcel cap remained interactive at every tested stream count. Sampled simulation times were generally below 4 ms and commonly below 1 ms once populations were draining. No browser errors or warnings were reported. The production build succeeds; Vite retains its existing advisory warning that the Three.js bundle exceeds 500 kB.

## Assessment

**Did removing the six hand-designed launch cases preserve convincing emergent convergence?**

Yes. Both seeds produced substantial capture at medium and high counts, while tangential and outward headings could resist or escape. The result is stronger evidence than universal success: the gravity well dominates compatible histories without erasing the consequences of generated initial conditions.

**Does Number of Streams behave as a meaningful control over fragmentation rather than merely producing more or fewer coloured packet trains?**

Yes. Total input remains mathematically constant. Two streams form a few substantial trains with little opportunity for cross-source interaction; twelve streams create thinner distributed arrivals, many more crossings, greater colour mixing and more varied local accumulations. The control changes organisation and interaction density, not material quantity.

All acceptance criteria were met. The results remain visually untidy, as expected for diagnostic particles, but avoid evenly spaced spokes, universal capture, universal congestion and continuously random spray.

## Recommendation

**A. Proceed to v0.2b-2 molten reconstruction.**

The variable source system is credible enough to freeze while material appearance is investigated. Its deterministic generator produces diverse, reproducible histories across a useful stream-count range without modifying the proven dynamics or depending on hand-designed convergence.
